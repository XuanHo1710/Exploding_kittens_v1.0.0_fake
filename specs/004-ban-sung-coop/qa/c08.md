## Cross-Check Findings

### [MAJOR] F1 — `skill_crit` Crit Damage (`critDmgMult`) and `skill_multishot` Weapon Energy Discount (`energyCostMult`) silently dropped before `weapons.ts`
- **Mismatch:**
  - `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts` specifies `skill_crit` (`common`..`legendary`, L2747–2807) with `secondaryValue` = `+15% / +25% / +40% / +60%` Crit Damage (`L2759, 2774, 2789, 2804`), and `skill_multishot` (`rare` / `epic` / `legendary`, L2704–2745) with `secondaryValue` = `0.05 / 0.08 / 0.12` Energy Cost reduction (`L2715, 2729, 2743`).
  - `server/src/game/bansung/pickups.ts` tracks and computes both fields cleanly in `SurvivalWeaponGachaMods` (`critDmgMult`, `energyCostMult`, L141–151), `syncSurvivalWeaponGachaMods` (L807–818), and `applySurvivalGachaSkillToHero` (L881–895).
  - **Drop point 1 (`world.ts`):** `syncWeaponAndHeroState` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts` L2841–2850) copies only 7 fields (`dmgMult`, `fireRateMult`, `extraMultishot`, `critChanceAdd`, `extraPierce`, `blastRadiusMult`, `reloadSpeedMul`) into `p.weaponState.gachaMods`, omitting `critDmgMult` and `energyCostMult` (`gachaEnergyCostMult` is forwarded only to Q-gadgets via `toGadgetPlayerActor`, L3438–3439).
  - **Drop point 2 (`weapons.ts`):** `BansungHeroGachaMods` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts` L245–253) lacks `critDmgMult` and `energyCostMult`. `getEffectiveEnergyCost` (L837–848) ignores Gacha energy reduction, and all 6 weapon crit dispatch sites (L1585, L1625, L1735, L1826, L1995, L2219) pass `critMult: spec.critMult` without `gacha?.critDmgMult`.
- **Why tests missed it:** `bansung-weapons.test.cjs` (`T08.4`, L1761–1803) checks `hero.gachaCritDmgMult` / `hero.gachaEnergyCostMult` in `pickups.ts` without firing through `world.ts` → `weapons.ts` to assert weapon crit damage or energy cost.

### [MAJOR] F2 — Bot Gacha auto-pick priority is inverted (`world.ts`)
- **Mismatch:**
  - `BOT_GACHA_CATEGORY_PRIORITY` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts` L503–511) assigns `weapon_upgrade: 0, skill_damage: 1, skill_hp: 2, skill_firerate: 3, skill_multishot: 4, skill_crit: 5, skill_mobility: 6`.
  - `pickBestGachaIdxForBot` (`world.ts` L3009–3024), invoked by `stepGachaTimers` after `BOT_GACHA_AUTO_PICK_MS = 800` (L2996), maximizes `prio` (`bestPrio = -1; if (prio > bestPrio || ...)`).
- **Impact:** Bots pick in reverse priority (`skill_mobility` > `skill_crit` > `skill_multishot` > `skill_firerate` > `skill_hp` > `skill_damage` > `weapon_upgrade`), actively avoiding weapon evolutions (`Mk.I -> Mk.IV`) and core damage/HP skills. (`bansung-net-security.test.cjs` `6b` runs with `{ bots: 0 }` and does not exercise `pickBestGachaIdxForBot`.)

### [MINOR] F3 — `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA` `FX_STATUS` argument mismatch (`world.ts` vs `render.ts`)
- **Mismatch:** `world.ts` `applyGachaPick` (L2963–2970) pushes `FX_STATUS` `(p.idx, BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA, clampedIdx, x, y)` where `c` is the chosen card slot (`0 | 1 | 2`), whereas `client/src/bansung/render.ts` (L2960–2977) reads `evt.c` as weapon evolution tier (`val >= 2 && val <= 4 ? BANSUNG_WEAPON_TIERS[normalizeWeaponTier(val)].auraColor : val === 1 ? '#38bdf8' : '#c084fc'`) and always displays `'EVOLVED!'` floating text even when picking a `skill_*` card or card index `0`/`1`.

### [MINOR] F4 — Unenforced `maxStacks` cap on late-game Gacha skill rolls (`shared/weapons.ts` / `pickups.ts`)
- **Mismatch:** `rollSurvivalGachaCards` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts` L3056) falls back to `pickSkillCategory(true)` when all 6 skills reach `maxStacks` (`12/12/8/6/8/8`) and all weapons are `Mk.IV`, and `applySurvivalGachaSkillToHero` (`server/src/game/bansung/pickups.ts` L826–941) has no `spec.maxStacks` guard — incrementing `g.skillStacks[card.category]` (L934) and stacking modifiers above `maxStacks`, causing `HotbarShop.tsx` (`L2008`) to render e.g. `Cấp 13/12`.

### [MINOR] F5 — `xp_orb_s/m` loot-table drops and `onXpGain` remain active in `'waves'` mode (`shared/enemies.ts` / `world.ts`)
- **Mismatch:** `world.ts` `recordMobDeath` (L3329–3345) gates `spawnEnemyXpOrbs` on `resolveBansungGameMode(this.config) === 'survival'`, but `this.pickupMgr.dropMobLoot(...)` (L3329) and crate breaks use `BANSUNG_LOOT_TABLES` (`shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts` L2373–2418: `pot`, `common`, `rare`, `mob_kill`, `elite_kill`), which include `xp_orb_s` and `xp_orb_m`. `world.ts` `stepWeaponsPickupsAndProps` (L1663–1667) also wires `onXpGain` unconditionally to `handleHeroXpGain`, allowing XP accumulation and level-up Gacha queues inside `'waves'` mode.

### [MINOR] F6 — Companion pet magnet skips XP orbs (`bot.ts`) & `skill_multishot:epic` description omission (`shared/weapons.ts`)
- `server/src/game/bansung/bot.ts` `isPickupNeededByOwner` (L2222–2236) returns `false` for `xp_orb_s/m/l`, so `runPetMagnet` (L2243–2300) does not pull XP orbs (even though `shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts` marks them `magnetized: true`, `needsFilter: 'always'`; `bot.ts` L2239 header only lists `hp, energy, armor, coin`).
- `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts` `skill_multishot.epic` (L2724–2729) sets `secondaryValue: 0.08`, while `desc`/`vnDesc` mention only `+2 Extra Projectiles` (omitting `-8% Energy Cost` unlike `rare` and `legendary`).

## Verified Invariants
1. **Shared `xp_orb_s/m/l` & Pickup Pipeline (`shared/enemies.ts` ↔ `pickups.ts`):**
   - `xp_orb_s` (kindIdx `8`, `14` XP), `xp_orb_m` (kindIdx `9`, `40` XP), `xp_orb_l` (kindIdx `10`, `145` XP) share `netKind: 'xp'`, `magnetized: true`, `needsFilter: 'always'`, `sharedCoopRatio: 0.4`, `ttlS: 35`, `spriteKey: 'pk_xp_orb'` (`shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts` L2308–2352).
   - `computePickupMagnetRadius` (`pickups.ts` L477–491) multiplies effective radius by `BANSUNG_XP_MAGNET_MULT` (`1.45`) for `netKind === 'xp'` and adds `gachaMagnetBonusPx` from `skill_mobility`.
   - `applyPickupCollection` (`pickups.ts` L1888–1918) awards full `amount` to the collector and `Math.ceil(amount * shareRatio)` (`40%`) to living, non-downed allies within `BANSUNG_XP_COOP_RADIUS` (`600` px) via `onXpGain` (verified by `T08.1`).
2. **Level-Up & Gacha Queue / Grace / Security (`world.ts` ↔ `security.ts` ↔ `client/store.ts` ↔ `HotbarShop.tsx`):**
   - `handleHeroXpGain` (`world.ts` L2860–2887) loops `while (p.xp >= p.xpToNext)` for multi-level spillover, queues `p.gachaQueue`, emits `FX_STATUS` `BANSUNG_STATUS_LEVELUP` (`7`) with `p.level`, opens choices immediately if idle, and maintains `p.gachaPending = p.gachaQueue + (p.gachaChoices ? 1 : 0)`.
   - `openNextGachaChoicesForHero` (`world.ts` L2889–2918) sets `GACHA_CHOICE_TIMEOUT_MS = 14_000` and grants `GACHA_POP_GRACE_MS = 1500` invulnerability/hit-iframes; `applyGachaPick` (L2920–2978) clears choices/timers, evolves weapon or applies skill, calls `recomputeHeroStats` + `syncWeaponAndHeroState`, and immediately pops queued rolls (`p.gachaQueue > 0`).
   - `validateBansungCommand` (`security.ts` L283–289) fails closed on non-integer/out-of-range `{ t: 'gacha', pick }` (`'invalid_gacha_pick'`) and prototype pollution, while `DEAD_OR_DOWNED_ALLOWED_CMDS` (L58–64) permits `'gacha'` while downed/dead (verified by `6a`).
   - `buildPrivateFor` (`world.ts` L3938–3944) omits `xp` when `0` and `level`/`xpToNext` at level `1` after initial frame; `normalizePrivateSlice` (`client/src/bansung/store.ts` L452–495) defaults omitted `xp` to `0`, `level` to `1`, and `xpToNext` to `getXpToNextLevel(level)`, preventing stale HUD XP after exact level-ups.
3. **Working `skill_*` Mechanics (`pickups.ts` → `world.ts` → `weapons.ts` / `gadgets.ts`):**
   - Hero HP/shield scaling (`world.ts` L863–864 `HERO_STAT_SCALE = 10`, base HP `50..70`, shield `40..70`) aligns with `skill_hp` (`+25..+120` HP, `+10..+60` shield).
   - Wired modifiers confirmed end-to-end: `skill_damage` (`dmgMult`), `skill_hp` (`maxHp`, `maxShield`, heal), `skill_firerate` (`fireRateMult` + `reloadSpeedMult` mapped to `reloadSpeedMul` at `world.ts` L2849), `skill_multishot` (`extraMultishot` + gadget energy discount via `toGadgetPlayerActor` L3438–3439), `skill_crit` (`critChanceAdd`, `extraPierce`), and `skill_mobility` (`moveSpeed`, magnet radius, `maxEnergy` + refill, and `dashCdMul` at `world.ts` L1565).
4. **Test Suite Verification (Read-Only Run):**
   - `node --test server/test/bansung-net-security.test.cjs`: **15/15 passed**, 0 failed, 0 skipped.
   - `node --test server/test/bansung-weapons.test.cjs`: **31/31 passed**, 0 failed, 0 skipped.

## Verdict: FAIL
`c08` fails cross-check due to **2 MAJOR** runtime mismatches (`F1`: `skill_crit` Crit Damage and `skill_multishot` weapon Energy Cost discount are dropped in `world.ts`/`weapons.ts`; `F2`: `BOT_GACHA_CATEGORY_PRIORITY` vs `pickBestGachaIdxForBot` inverts bot Gacha picks) alongside **4 MINOR** integration/UI discrepancies (`F3`–`F6`).
