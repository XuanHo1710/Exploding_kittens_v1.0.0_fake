## Cross-Check Findings

### 1. Dual independent target selection (`mobs.ts` vs `flocking.ts`) and windup/attack `facingLeft` overwrite in co-op `[severity: MAJOR]`

- **Evidence**:
  - `server/src/game/bansung/mobs.ts:2782` executes `stepMobTargetingAndDecisions` (`mobs.ts:1547-1573`), calling `pickBestTargetForMob` (`mobs.ts:1125-1157`) **every tick** with `score = dist + threatBias + (t.scoreOffset ?? 0)` (`MOB_THREAT_OFFSET_PX = -60` for `MOB_THREAT_DURATION_TICKS = 90`, `mobs.ts:135-138`), without crowd-load penalty or target hysteresis. It writes `mob.targetId`, `mob.aimAngle`, `mob.facingLeft`, and starts `windup` / `fuse` telegraphs (`mobs.ts:1525-1537, 1596-1853`).
  - `syncFlockProxyIn` (`mobs.ts:557-581`) and `flockTargets` construction (`mobs.ts:2791-2804`) do not synchronize `mob.targetId` into `mob.flockProxy.flock.targetId`, and do not forward `mob.threatTargetId` / `MOB_THREAT_OFFSET_PX` into `FlockTarget` (only `t.scoreOffset`).
  - `mobs.ts:2806` then invokes `stepEnemyFlocking` (`server/src/game/bansung/flocking.ts:1452-1554`), which runs an independent `selectTargetForEnemy` (`flocking.ts:503-556`) staggered every `BANSUNG_FLOCK_CONSTS.RETARGET_INTERVAL = 6` ticks (`shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts:704`) with `FLOCK_TARGET_LOAD_PENALTY = 48` (`FLOCK_BOMBER_LOAD_PENALTY = 20`, `flocking.ts:37-38`) and `FLOCK_HYSTERESIS_PX = 60` (`flocking.ts:39`).
  - Furthermore, while a mob is stationary in `windup`, `attack`, or `lunge`, `flocking.ts:1532-1534` and `clampAndSyncAnimation` (`flocking.ts:1431-1435`) overwrite `e.facingLeft = validTargets[tIdx].x < e.x` from flocking's `tIdx`, and `syncFlockProxyOut` (`mobs.ts:589`) copies that back to `mob.facingLeft` (packed as `BANSUNG_EFLAG_LEFT` in `toEnemyTuple` at `mobs.ts:2883`).
- **Impact**: In multi-player co-op (or whenever a recent attacker triggers `-60 px` threat bias), movement/flanking/ranged backoff (`flock.targetId`) and attack targeting/aim/telegraphs (`mob.targetId`) can lock onto different players—contravening `d34` shared `d36` targeting—and can flip `BANSUNG_EFLAG_LEFT` opposite the mob's actual `aimAngle` / fired projectile direction during `windup`/`attack`/`lunge`.

### 2. `DirectorMobSpawn.scaleMult` is dropped by `mobs.ts`, diverging hitbox radii from director spawn clearance & client sprite scale `[severity: MINOR]`

- **Evidence**:
  - `server/src/game/bansung/director.ts:136, 147, 266, 2230, 2257` computes `scaleMult = ELITE_SCALE_MULT * scaleBonus` (`1.2` for elites, `1.16` for evolved, `1.392` for evolved elites) and checks portal clearance with those multipliers in `spawnSurvivalBurst` / `spawnSquadBurst` (`director.ts:2141-2145, 2205`).
  - `DirectorSpawnPayload` (`mobs.ts:305-318`) omits `scaleMult`, `spawnMobFromDirector` (`mobs.ts:821-843`) drops it, and `spawnMob` (`mobs.ts:664-666`) keeps non-evolved elites at `1.0x` radius/mass while scaling `isEvolved` uniformly by `Math.max(stats.radius + 2, Math.round(stats.radius * 1.15))` and `mass * 1.15` (no extra scaling when `elite && evolved`).
  - `client/src/bansung/render.ts:2030, 3849` renders elites at `1.15`, evolved at `1.18`, and evolved+elite at `1.24`.
- **Impact**: Non-evolved elites and evolved+elite hybrids have smaller authoritative collision circles than both their director spawn-clearance checks and client-rendered sprites.

### 3. `spawnMob` initializes `flock` anchor at `(0, 0)` instead of `(clampedX, clampedY)` `[severity: MINOR]`

- **Evidence**: `server/src/game/bansung/mobs.ts:697` calls `createEnemyFlockState(numId, 0)` (so `y` defaults to `0` in `flocking.ts:268-280`), leaving `flock.anchorX = 0` and `flock.anchorY = 0` regardless of `(clampedX, clampedY)`.
- **Impact**: Because playable spawns have `x >= 48` and `y >= 96`, `Math.hypot(e.x - flock.anchorX, e.y - flock.anchorY) >= 96 >= FLOCK_MIN_STUCK_MOVE_PX` (`6`, `flocking.ts:52, 1258`) on the first `FLOCK_STUCK_CHECK_TICKS` (`15` ticks) check, disabling stuck-detour sign-flip recovery for the first `0.5 s` after spawn until `anchorX`/`anchorY` refresh at tick 15 (`flocking.ts:1263-1264`).

### 4. `world.ts` passes mixed-entity `this.actorGrid` into `stepMobs` as `enemyGrid` without boss/gate Boids separation `[severity: MINOR]`

- **Evidence**:
  - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2099` passes `enemyGrid: this.actorGrid`, into which `rebuildSpatialGrid` (`world.ts:1726-1830`) inserts mobs plus bosses (`boss_beholder`, `boss_colossus`, `boss_overlord`), Colossus drones, and `portal_rush` gates (`PORTAL_GATE_SPATIAL_BASE + gate.id`) under `LAYER_ENEMY`.
  - In `stepEnemyFlocking` (`flocking.ts:1507-1508`), `computeBoidsForces` (`flocking.ts:831-845`) queries `activeGrid.queryEnemies(..., MAX_NEIGHBORS = 10)` and skips non-proxy IDs (`lookupEnemyIndex(oid) < 0`). Hard separation (`flocking.ts:1629-1633`) is unaffected because `world.ts` omits `enemyOnlyGrid: true`, triggering `internalEnemyGrid.rebuild(enemies)`.
- **Impact**: Mobs get no soft Boids separation around bosses, drones, or portal gates, and nearby non-mob `LAYER_ENEMY` actors consume slots from the 10-neighbor Boids cap.
