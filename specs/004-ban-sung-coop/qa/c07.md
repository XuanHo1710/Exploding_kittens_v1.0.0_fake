## Cross-Check Findings

### 1. `[BLOCKER]` `heal_drone` HP & Shield healing never persists in `world.ts`
- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:2039-2060`, `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1932-1944, 1978-1984, 3427-3444`
- **Mismatch**: `world.ts` passes `gadgetPlayers = this.players.map((p) => this.toGadgetPlayerActor(p))` into `stepGadgetWorld(...)`. `toGadgetPlayerActor` returns a fresh shallow-spread copy (`{ ...(p as BansungCombatTarget), ... }`). In `stepHealDrone`, `bestPlayer.hp += hpAdd` and `bestPlayer.shield += shieldAdd` mutate that throwaway copy (`gadgets.ts:2048-2049`). While `world.ts:1978-1984` supplies `onEnergyGrant` to sync energy onto `this.players[gActor.idx]`, there is no HP/shield writeback callback or post-step copyback.
- **Impact**: Heal Drone emits green `FX_DMG` (`BANSUNG_DMG_HEAL`) popups and heal audio every 10 ticks while leaving player `hp`, `shield`, and `armor` unchanged.

### 2. `[BLOCKER]` Gadget damage to Bosses, Iron Drones, Overlord Pylons/WeakPoints, and Portal Gates is lost in `world.ts`
- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1935-1958, 3446-3486`, `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:1131-1147`
- **Mismatch**: `collectGadgetEnemies()` pushes live object references only for regular mobs (`out.push(m as GadgetEnemyActor)` at `world.ts:3450`). For every `auxTargets` entry (`boss_beholder` `sand_boss`, `portal_gate`, `overlord_pylon`, `overlord_wp`) and every `activeBosses` entry (`boss_colossus`, `d` = `iron_drone`, `boss_overlord`), it pushes fresh shallow-spread copies (`{ ...aux.target }`, `{ ...entry.boss }`, `{ ...d }`). `applyPairDamageToEnemy` mutates the temporary copy's `.hp`, and `onDamage` (`world.ts:1947-1958`) only updates player stats and checks `mobMgr.mobByNumId`.
- **Impact**: Stun bombs, cluster bombs/sub-bomblets, turret shots, thunder splash, and turret expiry blasts inflict **0 actual HP damage** on all bosses, iron drones, Overlord pylons/weakpoints, and portal gates (while emitting false hit popups and inflating `damageDealt`/`bossDamage`).

### 3. `[BLOCKER]` Projectile ID namespace collision (`1..N`) across `bulletPool`, `gadgetState.projectiles`, and `gadgetState.turretShots`
- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:1167,1252`, `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:642,645,932,1374,1732`, `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3411,3795-3841`, `client/src/bansung/store.ts:547-568`, `client/src/bansung/vfx.ts:2812,3361`
- **Mismatch**: `world.ts` offsets `mobMgr.projectiles` (`500_000 + mp.id`) and `bossProjectiles` (`700_000 + ...`), whereas `BansungBulletPool` (`nextId = 1`), `gadgetState.projectiles` (`nextProjId = 1`), and `gadgetState.turretShots` (`nextShotId = 1`) all generate overlapping IDs starting at `1` and are inserted raw into `this.bulletBuckets`. Client `decodeBullets` (`store.ts:556`) keys a `Map<number, SnapBullet>` by `id`, and `BansungVfxController` (`vfx.ts:2812,3361`) keys `bulletSlotById` by `b.id`.
- **Impact**: Concurrent hero bullets, thrown gadget bombs, and turret bolts with matching integer IDs overwrite/drop each other in snapshot `Map`s and corrupt client tracer origin/interpolation state.

### 4. `[MAJOR]` Turret decoy aggro (`canEnemyAggroTurret`), mob-vs-turret projectile resolution, and `stun_bomb` accuracy debuff are unwired
- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:1235-1244,2218-2235`, `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1720-1724,1959-1964,2059-2064,3126-3148,3205-3208`, `server/src/game/bansung/mobs.ts:2262-2301,2526,2575-2576`
- **Mismatch**:
  - `world.ts:1721` registers turrets in `actorGrid` (`LAYER_PET`) and `resolveCombatTarget` (`L3126-3148`) + `reconcileAuxAndBossTargets` (`L3205-3208`) include turret `auxTargets` scaffolding, **but `world.ts:2059-2064` populates `mobTargets` exclusively from `this.players`**. Neither `world.ts` nor `mobs.ts` ever invokes `canEnemyAggroTurret`, and `mobs.ts:2526,2576` (`buildTargetByNumId(targets)`) returns `undefined` if a mob projectile hits a turret's `SpatialActor` in `targetGrid`. Turrets take zero mob damage or aggro.
  - `detonateStunBomb` writes `enemy.accuracyDebuffTicks = 45` (`0.65` mult per `BANSUNG_GADGETS.stun_bomb`) and forwards them via `onStunInterrupt`, but neither `world.ts:1959-1964` nor `mobs.ts` reads `accuracyDebuffTicks`/`accuracyDebuffMult`.

### 5. `[MAJOR]` `FX_BLAST` code collision & missing `onSfx` callback degrade gadget audio and Thunder Turret VFX
- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:76-81,1763-1768,1925-1932`, `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:117-123`, `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1367-1385,1939-1985`, `client/src/bansung/vfx.ts:3136-3147`, `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:618-625,1781-1790`
- **Mismatch**:
  - `gadgets.ts` emits `FX_BLAST` `BANSUNG_BLAST_TURRET_THUNDER = 3` and `BANSUNG_BLAST_TURRET_EXPIRE = 4`. Those codes collide with `BANSUNG_BLAST_KINDS` (`mine: 3, fireball: 4`). Consequently `vfx.ts:3136-3144` renders turret thunder overcharge impacts as `'steam'` (gray `FxColor.Dust`, no electric arcs) and turret expiry blasts as `'fireball'`, while `sound.ts:1788` plays `'explosion_sm'` (`radius = 48 < 95`) instead of lightning/stun SFX.
  - `stepAutoTurret` emits `FX_MUZZLE` with `dep.id >= INITIAL_DEPLOYABLE_ID (1000)` and `projCfg.kindId` (`9` or `14`) (`gadgets.ts:91,1755-1762`), which triggers the turret branches in `sound.ts:1744-1747` and `vfx.ts:3049-3055`; however, `world.ts:857` also allocates `hero.actorId = 1000 + idx` (`>= 1000`), creating an overlapping `>= 1000` namespace in `FX_MUZZLE` between heroes and deployables.
  - `world.ts` never provides `onSfx` to `activateGadget` (`L1373`) or `stepGadgetWorld` (`L1939`), and client input/hotbar (`input.ts:983-996`, `HotbarShop.tsx:2311-2321`) emits no local deploy SFX. Thus deploy sounds (`gadget_throw`, `gadget_turret_deploy`, `gadget_drone_deploy`) defined in `BANSUNG_GADGETS` and aliased in `sound.ts:618,621,624` never play.

### 6. `[MAJOR]` Wave-clear `fullRestock` overfills gadget charges past `maxCharges`, while mobile Touch Skill button locks out multi-charge bombs during recharge
- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2668-2669`, `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:662-669,897-900`, `client/src/components/BansungBoard.tsx:739`, `client/src/components/bansung/HotbarShop.tsx:1256-1257`
- **Mismatch**:
  - `world.ts:2668` executes `p.gadgetSlot.charges = Math.max(1, p.gadgetSlot.charges + 1)` without clamping to `getGadgetSpec(p.gadgetSlot.id).maxCharges`, allowing `auto_turret`/`heal_drone` (`maxCharges: 1`) and bombs (`maxCharges: 2`) to stack above `maxCharges` across waves (`tickGadgetSlot` only stops incrementing; it does not clamp down).
  - `BansungBoard.tsx:739` sets `disabled={!me || me.gadget.cdMs > 0 || me.gadget.charges <= 0}`. After throwing 1 charge of a 2-charge bomb (`charges === 1`, `cdMs > 0` up to `8000`/`12000`ms), the mobile skill button disables the remaining charge, inconsistent with `HotbarShop.tsx:1257` (`blocked = !hasCharge && gadget.cdMs > 0`) and desktop Q hotkey (`input.ts:983-996`).

### 7. `[MINOR]` Client rendering, manifest animation & fallback placement discrepancies
- **Heal drone aura radius mismatch** (`client/src/bansung/render.ts:3607-3614` vs `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2197,2211,2216`): `render.ts` hardcodes a `110` px dashed green ring instead of `190` px (`BANSUNG_GADGETS.heal_drone.radius`) / `150` px (`coopBonusRadius`, `energyPulseRadius`).
- **Turret facing & non-looping sprite clamping** (`server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:2295-2305,2363-2382`, `client/src/bansung/render.ts:1540-1543,3804-3815`, `client/src/bansung/vfx.ts:3401-3410`, `client/src/bansung/sprites.ts:837-840`, `database-ban-sung/manifest.json:50-71,1859-1881,6555-6571`):

  - `auto_turret`/`gadget_auto_turret` (`frames: 4, fps: 15, loop: false`) and `thunderball` (`frames: 16, fps: 18, loop: false`) are driven by global `nowSec`, so `resolveFrameIndex` clamps them permanently to their final frame after `~0.27s` / `~0.89s`.
- **Unused `spawnBombFx`, `cluster_sub` radius & `gadget_bombs` sprite key** (`client/src/bansung/vfx.ts:389-391,1272,2632,3354`, `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:704,731`, `database-ban-sung/manifest.json:1882`):

  - `BOMB_RADII.cluster_sub` (`Math.round(105 * 0.58) = 61`) disagrees with `BANSUNG_GADGETS.cluster_bomb.cluster.radius` (`68`), and `BANSUNG_PROJ_ANIMS` maps `15/16` to `p_stun_canister`/`cluster_bomb` instead of `gadget_bombs`.
- **Unreachable `activateGadget` no-coord defaults** (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1358-1365`, `gadgets.ts:873-889`, `HotbarShop.tsx:2320`): `world.ts` synthesizes `targetX/Y` `160px` ahead when `cmd.x === undefined` (e.g., hotbar click `emitCommand({ t: 'gadget' })`), bypassing `activateGadget`'s `deployOffsetPx = 28` (`auto_turret`) and behind-player `-followBehindPx = 24` (`heal_drone`) defaults. (`world.ts:1381-1384,1548-1551,3442` also hardcode zero extra charges/deployables and `cdMult: 1`, though no active perks/gacha currently modify gadget CD/charges.)

## Verified Invariants

- **Canonical ID & Hotbar Asset Alignment**: `BANSUNG_GADGET_IDS` (`types.ts:77`) matches `BANSUNG_GADGETS` (`weapons.ts:2069-2223`), `HERO_DEFAULT_GADGET` (`world.ts:490-495`), and `BANSUNG_GADGET_ICON_META` vs `manifest.json` dimensions/paths (`HotbarShop.tsx:155-160`, `manifest.json:1859-1965`). `BANSUNG_PROJECTILE_KINDS` indices (`9, 14, 15, 16, 17`) and `BANSUNG_PROP_IDS` indices (`turret: 6`, `drone: 7`) align across `shared`, `gadgets.ts`, `store.ts`, `render.ts`, and `vfx.ts`.
- **Command Security & Life-State Gating**: `DEAD_OR_DOWNED_ALLOWED_CMDS` excludes `'gadget'` (`security.ts:58-62`), `validateBansungCommand` clamps finite `(x, y)` and rejects non-finite coordinates (`security.ts:243-260`), `input.ts:972,983-996` gates skill input on `!this.downed`, and `world.ts:1357` + `gadgets.ts:854-855` enforce alive/non-downed/non-stunned checks.
- **Drone Energy Pulse, Downed Tether & Regular Mob Stuns**: `onEnergyGrant` (`world.ts:1978-1984`) grants Heal Drone pulse energy cleanly; `getDroneDownedSupport` -> `p.droneTethered` (`world.ts:2489-2492`) -> `stepDownedAndRevive` (`bot.ts:944,1017`) correctly applies `0.5x` bleedout rate and `+0.25` revive speed; `onStunInterrupt` -> `stunMob` (`world.ts:1959-1963`) cancels fuse/channels on regular mobs; `hadGadgetPropsLastTick` (`world.ts:4007-4016`) properly flushes cleared deployables to `store.ts:1123-1128`.
- **Unit Test Coverage**: `node --test server/test/bansung-weapons.test.cjs` passed 31/31 tests (`0` failures, `0` skipped); combined bansung suite (`bansung-weapons`, `bansung-physics`, `bansung-net-security`, `bansung-assets`, `bansung-ai-waves`) passed 96/96 subtests.
