## Cross-Check Findings

### [BLOCKER]
1. **Timestamp domain mismatch (`performance.now()` vs `visualTimeMs`) corrupts `HeroDashTracker` lifecycle and risks `hitstop` cooldown lockout**
   - `client/src/bansung/render.ts:3301-3302` steps `bansungMotion.step(rawDtMs, nowMs)` using monotonic `nowMs` (`performance.now()`, `render.ts:3291-3292`), then reads `const visualNowMs = bansungMotion.getVisualTimeMs()` (`0`-based elapsed ms excluding hitstop).
   - `render.ts:3375` passes `nowMs: visualNowMs` into `bansungMotion.syncHeroDash(...)`, setting `tracker.lastSeenMs = visualNowMs` (`motion.ts:1279`).
   - Inside `step()`, `stepDashTrackers(visualDtS, nowMs)` (`motion.ts:1804, 1872`) evaluates `nowMs - tracker.lastSeenMs > MOTION_CFG.DASH_TRACKER_PRUNE_MS` (`4000`) against `performance.now()`. Whenever `performance.now() - visualNowMs > 4000` (always true >4s after page load), **every non-dashing `HeroDashTracker` is deleted every frame** (`motion.ts:1882`) and recreated in `syncHeroDash` (`motion.ts:1188, 1260`), causing per-frame GC allocation and resetting `lastSeq`, `wasDashing`, and `hasPos`.
   - `render.ts:3344` also passes `nowMs: visualNowMs` to `processBansungFxEvents` -> `bansungMotion.triggerExplosionJuice` (`render.ts:2922`, `vfx.ts:2789`) -> `triggerHitstop(dur, nowMs)` (`motion.ts:1692, 1702`). If any caller omits `nowMs` (`nowDefault()` -> `performance.now()`), `lastHitstopAt` jumps to page uptime and suppresses `visualNowMs`-based hitstops via `nowMs - lastHitstopAt < 140`.

### [MAJOR]
2. **Double camera trauma shake in `render.ts` stacked on top of `store.ts`'s independent shake**
   - `motion.ts:1822-1825` assigns `this.camera.shakeX = sx`, `this.camera.shakeY = sy`, and `this.camera.offsetX = this.camera.rx + sx`, `this.camera.offsetY = this.camera.ry + sy`.
   - `render.ts:3398-3400` computes `totalCamX = o.cam.x + o.cam.shakeX + juice.shakeX + juice.offsetX` (and analogously for `totalCamY`), applying `bansungMotion`'s trauma shake **twice** (`2 * sx + rx`, `2 * sy + ry`) while also adding `store.ts`'s separate `cam.shakeX`/`cam.shakeY` (`store.ts:2091-2101, 2349-2363`).

3. **Core `d20` / `d21` / `d22` / `d42` motion, recoil, crosshair, and FX hooks are uncalled across `client/src`**
   - Cross-file search across `client/src/**/*.{ts,tsx}` confirms **zero external callers** for:
     - `triggerShotJuice`, `triggerGunRecoil`, `triggerHitstop`, `triggerCritHitstop`, `triggerHitMarker`, `triggerDashCameraKick`, `addCameraTrauma`, and `predictLocalDash`. `vfx.ts:3037-3081` consumes `FX_MUZZLE` only for flashes/casings without calling recoil/juice, and `anim.ts:1413` calls `bansungMotion.readGunRecoil(player.id, player.aim)` every frame on an empty `gunSprings` map (so recoil kick/snap, shot trauma, crit hitstop, and local dash prediction never trigger).
     - `drawCrosshair`, `setCrosshairWeapon`, `setCrosshairContext`, and `triggerDryFirePulse`: `render.ts:4224-4235` renders static `drawAimReticle` (`render.ts:2674-2722`, fixed `spread = 6`, `arm = 5`) with no bloom, hit/crit X markers, reload bar, or dry-fire flash.
     - `bansungFx.spawnDamageNumber` (`particles.ts:1699`), `bansungFx.setCasingBounceHook` (`particles.ts:845`), `bansungMotion.setBiome` (`motion.ts:1079`), and `clearGhostTintCache` (`motion.ts:615`): `render.ts:2887` uses its own local `spawnDamageNumber` (`render.ts:2728`), and `sound.ts` never wires casing bounce audio.
   - Furthermore, `anim.ts:1413` calls `bansungMotion.readGunRecoil(player.id, player.aim)` without the optional reusable `out` parameter (`motion.ts:1583-1600`), allocating a fresh `{ kickPos, snapAngle, pivotOffsetX, pivotOffsetY }` object per hero per frame.

4. **`MAX_DECAL_CHUNKS = 16` without LRU starves chunk baking on the `4224×2688` arena**
   - With `BANSUNG_MAP_W = 4224`, `BANSUNG_MAP_H = 2688` (`shared/src/bansung/maps.ts`) and `DECAL_CHUNK_SIZE = 512` (`particles.ts:35`), the arena spans `9 × 6 = 54` chunks (matching `CHUNK_GRID_FAST_SLOTS = 64` at `particles.ts:72`).
   - `particles.ts:37, 2534` enforces a hard `MAX_DECAL_CHUNKS = 16` cap with **no LRU eviction**. Once 16 distinct 512×512 tiles receive a static decal, `getOrCreateChunk` returns `-1` for all remaining 38 tiles; `spawnDecal` (`particles.ts:1608-1632`) and `settleDecalAtIndex` (`particles.ts:2490-2512`) then fall back to the 384-slot `SETTLED_TTL_MS = 12_000` fading buffer.

5. **Degraded-mode additive composite switch in `particles.ts` is overridden by `render.ts` PASS 5**
   - `particles.ts:1932-1934` skips setting `'lighter'` when `this.degradedMsLeft > 0`, but does not explicitly set `ctx.globalCompositeOperation = 'source-over'`.
   - `render.ts:4240` sets `ctx.globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter'` before calling `bansungFx.drawLayer(ctx, FxLayer.Additive, ...)` (`render.ts:4244`), so additive particles continue blending additively even during the 3-frame `>24ms` frame-drop guard (`particles.ts:46-48, 900-904`).

### [MINOR]
6. **`spawnDecal` `z` vs `scale` positional argument bug in `render.ts`**
   - `render.ts:2910-2915` (`FX_DEATH` blood/oil splats) and `render.ts:2939` (`FX_STATUS` `BANSUNG_STATUS_CRATE` rubble) pass `1` as the 5th argument (`z`, world height) to `bansungFx.spawnDecal(kind, x, y, angle, z, opts)` (`particles.ts:1571-1578`).
   - Because `z > 0` forces `airborne = true` (`particles.ts:1599-1605`), blood/oil/rubble briefly drop from `z = 1` instead of baking immediately and spawn an unintended `FxColor.SandSpeck` dust particle on ground hit (`particles.ts:983-994`).

7. **Dash puff hints discarded (`render.ts` vs `motion.ts`) & Miragine dash ghost scale mismatch**
   - `motion.ts:1227, 1857-1865` enqueue dash puff hints with `count: 7` (takeoff) / `count: 2` (landing) and biome-matched `color`; `render.ts:3380-3390` spawns only **1** `FxKind.Dust` particle per hint (ignoring `puff.count` and `puff.color`) with `size: 4`.
   - `anim.ts:997-998` scales `nativeFacing === 'left'` Miragine fallback hero sprites at `1` (`isMiragineLeft ? 1 : hero.pixelScale`), whereas `motion.ts:773-774` defaults `drawDashGhost`'s `pixelScale` to `hero.pixelScale` (`1.5`), and `render.ts:4065-4074` passes `nativeFacing: meta.nativeFacing` without `pixelScale`—rendering Miragine dash ghosts `1.5×` larger than the hero.

8. **Redundant post-`step` hitstop time scaling, missing `reducedFlash` check, and per-call spread allocations**
   - `render.ts:3301-3308` queries `bansungMotion.getCameraJuice().hitstopRemMs > 0` *after* `bansungMotion.step(rawDtMs, nowMs)` (applying `0.15×` dilation to `bansungFx`/`bansungVfx` cleanly except on the final partial hitstop frame where `hitstopRemMs` already drained to `0`); additionally, `render.ts:2920-2925` calls `triggerScreenFlash` and `triggerExplosionJuice` without checking `!opts?.reducedFlash`.
   - `motion.ts:1323, 1337, 1365` (`sampleHeroMotion`, `samplePlayerTransform`) construct temporary `{ ...input }` / literal input objects per call (though hot hero rendering calls `sampleHeroMotionTransform` directly via `anim.ts:829` — which itself omits an `out` object).
   - `render.ts:3366` falls back to `'cyber_ranger'` (not in `BANSUNG_HERO_IDS` at `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:40`; canonical ID is `'ranger'`), mitigated safely by `getBansungHero` falling back to `'mascot'`.

## Verified Invariants
- **TypeScript compilation**: `cd client && npx tsc -p tsconfig.json --noEmit` passes with exit code `0`.
- **Weapon recoil preset coverage**: `WEAPON_RECOIL_PROFILES` (`motion.ts:184-232`) maps all 15 canonical `BANSUNG_WEAPONS` entries without missing keys (`pistol`/`rifle`/`smg`/`shotgun`/`railgun` from `RECOIL_PROFILES` at `motion.ts:111-182`, plus scaled `explosive`, `melee`, and `energy`/`magic` profiles matching `d21` fields).
- **Zero-GC internal TypedArray pools in `particles.ts`**: Fixed SoA pools (`PARTICLE_CAP = 1024`, `DECAL_CAP = 384`, `TEXT_CAP = 128`), O(1) swap-and-pop removal (`particles.ts:916-924`), bounded slot eviction (`EVICT_PROBE_COUNT = 16`, `DECAL_EVICT_PROBE = 24` at `particles.ts:69-70, 2404-2469`), pre-interned damage/heal/crit strings up to `999` (`INTERN_NUM_MAX = 999` mapped to `BANSUNG_DMG_*` bitflags at `particles.ts:67, 1699-1739`), and `CASING_PHYSICS` parameters (`particles.ts:54-65`) match `d22` / `d42`.
- **Internal motion pools & reduced-motion math in `motion.ts`**: Pre-allocated 64 dash ghosts (`GHOST_POOL_SIZE = BANSUNG_DASH.GHOST_POOL_SIZE`), 32 dust rings (`DUST_RING_POOL_SIZE = 32`), 64 puff hints (`DUST_PUFF_QUEUE_SIZE = 64`), pooled `GunSpringState` free-list (`motion.ts:1520-1543`), `140ms` hitstop cooldown (`HITSTOP_COOLDOWN_MS = 140`), `0.25×` reduced-shake scale + hitstop zeroing (`motion.ts:78, 1084-1089`), and `trauma^2 * MAX_SHAKE_PX` deterministic shake (`motion.ts:1813-1815`) align with `d20` / `d21`.
- **Lifecycle reset & reduced-motion wiring**: `client/src/components/BansungBoard.tsx:245-246, 270, 301` resets both `bansungMotion.reset()` and `bansungFx.reset(mapW, mapH)` on mount/teardown and forwards `reducedMotion` to `bansungMotion.setReducedScreenShake(reducedMotion)`.

---

## Verdict: **FAIL**
While `motion.ts` and `particles.ts` internally implement the `d20`/`d21`/`d22`/`d42` contracts cleanly and pass `tsc --noEmit`, cross-module integration fails on a `BLOCKER` timestamp-domain bug in `render.ts` (`performance.now()` vs `visualTimeMs` deleting every idle `HeroDashTracker` every frame after 4s), plus `MAJOR` defects including doubled trauma shake, disconnected shot/recoil/hitstop/crosshair hooks, un-evicted `16`-chunk decal starvation on the `4224×2688` arena, and an ineffective degraded-mode additive composite guard.
