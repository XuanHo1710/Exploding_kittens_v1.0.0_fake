# QA Audit Report — c10: Bosses AI & Arena Clarity

## Scope

Static cross-module audit of the three co-op boss implementations against arena geometry (`4224x2688`), wave/survival spawning, server combat/network framing, client rendering/HUD, and test coverage (no runtime test execution performed in this audit):

- Boss AI & state modules: `server/src/game/bansung/boss-sand.ts`, `server/src/game/bansung/boss-colossus.ts`, `server/src/game/bansung/boss-overlord.ts`
- Shared arena, enemy & transport contracts: `shared/src/bansung/maps.ts`, `shared/src/bansung/types.ts`, `shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts`
- Server orchestration & damage pipeline: `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts`, `server/src/game/bansung/director.ts`, `server/src/game/bansung/damage.ts`, `server/src/game/game.ts`
- Client state, VFX & HUD consumers: `client/src/bansung/store.ts`, `client/src/bansung/render.ts`, `client/src/bansung/vfx.ts`, `client/src/components/bansung/HudBars.tsx`
- Existing tests inspected: `server/test/bansung-ai-waves.test.cjs`, `server/test/bansung-net-security.test.cjs`

  - `client/src/bansung/render.ts:3464-3502, 3690-3695, 3771-3789, 3976-4025, 4248-4268`
- **Details**:
  - `BansungFrame` (`{ t, ms, w, wp, rem, p, e?, b?, pr?, pk?, fx?, bs?, me?, srv?, full? }`) and `BansungBossFrame` (`{ id, kind, x, y, hp, maxHp, phase, state, aimQ, telegraph?, stagger?, maxStagger? }`) have no fields for `telegraphs`, `weakPoints`, `pylons`, or `laserLanes`.
  - `world.ts:3666` computes `this.telegraphs = this.viewTelegraphs();`, and `frameFor()` (`world.ts:4031-4048`) drops it; `sendBansungFrames()` (`game.ts:1919-1930`) only transmits `w.frameFor(p.id)`. `viewTelegraphs()`, `toColossusBossView()`, `toOverlordView()`, and `toDetailedView()` have zero transport callers across `server/src/`, `shared/src/`, and `client/src/`.
  - `store.ts:1136-1151` populates `SnapBoss` exclusively from `frame.bs`, leaving `rawBoss`/`frameBoss` fields (`telegraphs`, `weakPoints`, `pylons`, `laserLanes`) expected by `render.ts` permanently `undefined`.
  - Furthermore, `world.ts:1165-1172` (`viewTelegraphs()`) omits `boss_beholder` altogether, and `world.ts:2258-2322` (`stepActiveBosses()`) calls Sand Beholder step with only `{ onSpawnProjectile, onSpawnAdd, onHeroHit, onObstacleHit }`, omitting `onTelegraphCircle`, `onBossBeam`, `onShockwave`, `onPushback`, and `onSfx` and discarding the return value (`void res;`). - **Locations**:
  - `shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts:110-125, 842-858`

  - In `render.ts:3516-3561`, iterating `store.enemies` sees `BANSUNG_ENEMIES[e.enemyId].isBoss === true` (`boss_colossus`), matches `bossRenderView.kind === e.enemyId` (`L3519`), overwrites `bossRenderView` flags (`hitFlash`, `stunned`, `burning`, `shielded`, `facingLeft`) with the drone's flags, and hits `continue;` (`L3527`).

### [BLOCKER] 3. Gadgets deal zero damage to all bosses and boss sub-targets

- **Locations**: `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1939, 3452-3485`
- **Details**: `collectGadgetEnemies()` (`world.ts:3452-3485`) spreads `{ ...(aux.target) }`, `{ ...entry.boss }`, and `{ ...d }` into fresh shallow copies rather than passing live references. `stepGadgetWorld` (`world.ts:1939` — `stun_bomb`, `cluster_bomb`, `auto_turret`) mutates `.hp` on those discarded copies.
- **Impact**: Player gadgets deal zero persistent damage to Sand Beholder, Dread Colossus, Iron Drones, Overlord, WeakPoints, or EMP Pylons.

### [BLOCKER] 4. Boss HP co-op, difficulty, and endless multipliers are double-applied in `spawnWaveBoss()`

- **Locations**:
  - `shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts:1452-1460, 1685-1688`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/director.ts:1333, 1383, 1402, 1966`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2719-2783`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-sand.ts:409-416`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-colossus.ts:1184-1200`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-overlord.ts:609-623`
- **Details**:
  - `director.ts` computes `this.bossHpMult = computeBossHpMult(nPlayers, difficulty, endlessTier)` (`(1 + 0.65*(n-1)) * d.bossHp * (1 + 0.35*endlessTier)`) and passes it as `out.spawnBoss.hpMult` (and in Survival via `schedule.bossHpMult` at `enemies.ts:1685-1688` / `director.ts:1966`).
  - `spawnWaveBoss()` (`world.ts:2722-2774`) then passes BOTH `(playerCount, difficulty, endlessTier)` AND `hpMult: spec.hpMult` into `createSandBeholderBoss`, `createDreadColossus`, and `createOverlordBoss` — each of which multiplies base boss HP (and drones/weak-points) by the co-op, difficulty, and endless multipliers a second time.
  - Additionally, in Survival cycle 3+, `world.ts:2719` computes `endlessTier` from synthetic survival wave `cycle*4 + stageIdx + 1`, stacking an extra endless tier multiplier.
- **Impact**: Boss, drone, and weak-point HP scales quadratically instead of linearly (e.g., 4P Normal gets `2.95 * 2.95 = 8.70x` HP instead of `2.95x`; 4P Hell gets `5.1625^2 = 26.65x`).

### [MAJOR] 5. Broken compact-fallback telegraphs & client/server scale mismatches

- **Locations**:
  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-sand.ts:154-164, 1372-1386, 1993-2011`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-colossus.ts:109, 822-823, 968-969, 2196-2221`
  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-overlord.ts:1123` (`enemies.ts:919-920`)
  - `client/src/bansung/render.ts:3715-3775`
- **Details**:
  - **Sand Beholder (`boss_beholder`)**: `BEHOLDER_TO_UNIT_STATE` (`boss-sand.ts:154-164`) maps `volley_cast`, `beam_windup`, `beam_sweep`, and `enrage_roar` all to `BANSUNG_STATE_ATK`, and `computeTelegraphPct` (`boss-sand.ts:1993-2011`) returns `> 0` during both `volley_cast` (before first volley ring) and `beam_windup`. As a result, `render.ts:3731-3750` draws a false 640px laser sight line during radial thorn `volley_cast` windups with no volley telegraph. Separately, `render.ts:3720-3730` draws the `slam_leap` warning ring centered on the moving airborne boss `(bossRenderView.x, bossRenderView.y)` instead of `(slamTargetX, slamTargetY)` (`boss-sand.ts:1372-1386`).
  - **Dread Colossus (`boss_colossus`)**: `DASH_TELEGRAPH_LEN = 480` (`boss-colossus.ts:109, 968-969`) and `render.ts:3756` (`const len = 480`) overshoot Colossus's actual max dash travel `680 * 18 / 30 = 408px` (`boss-colossus.ts:822-823`, `+72px` false warning zone). `boss-colossus.ts:2196-2199` also maps `DASH_WINDUP`, `DASH_CHARGE`, and `DASH_RECOVER` all to `BANSUNG_STATE_DASH`, keeping the full `480px` dash corridor box rendered while charging and recovering (`render.ts:3754`). Moreover, `toColossusBossFrame` (`boss-colossus.ts:2221`) sets `telegraph = boss.telegraphs[0].prog` (`0..255` scale) whereas `boss-sand.ts`, `boss-overlord.ts`, and `render.ts:3715` (`clamp(bossRenderView.telegraph, 0, 100) / 100`) treat `frame.bs.telegraph` as `0..100`.
  - **Overlord Pylon (`chargeQ`)**: `render.ts:3775` divides `chargeQ` by `255`, whereas `boss-overlord.ts:1123` / `enemies.ts:919-920` emit `chargeQ` in `0..100`, capping the charge ratio at `~39%` and never reaching the `> 0.8` white flash threshold even if pylons were sent.

## Verified Invariants

- **Arena `4224x2688` bounds & center parity**: `BANSUNG_MAP_W = 4224`, `BANSUNG_MAP_H = 2688`, `BANSUNG_ARENA_CX = 2112`, `BANSUNG_ARENA_CY = 1368`, `BANSUNG_PLAYABLE_BOUNDS = { x0: 48, y0: 96, x1: 4176, y1: 2640 }`, and `BANSUNG_BOSS_CLEAR_RADIUS = 260` (`shared/src/bansung/maps.ts:19-44`).
- **Wave center & Survival pedestal spawn logic**: `director.ts:1369-1407, 1954-1972, 2006-2040` spawns Wave bosses cleanly at `arena.bossPad` (`[2112, 1368]`) via `resolveSpawnClear`; Survival `boss_hunt` (`pickSurvivalBossSpawnPos`) chooses `[2112, 1368]` when all living players are `>= 300px` away, otherwise selecting the farthest unobstructed shrine among the 5 `PEDESTAL_ANCHORS` (`[1056, 1368]`, `[3168, 1368]`, `[2112, 528]`, `[1296, 2088]`, `[2928, 2088]`).
- **Cross-map target acquisition & obstacle clamping**: `world.ts:2202-2254` provides full living-player target lists without aggro-radius truncation so all three bosses roam and pursue across `4224x2688` (Sand Beholder prefers `[190, 280]` with `beamRange = 640px` / `slamTrigger = 220px`; Colossus pursues nearest and dashes across `[120, 560]px`; Overlord orbits at `260px`, spans `laserMaxRayLen = 2600px` half-rays (`5200px` total, exceeding the `4224x2688` diagonal), and anchors P2 cross-lanes on `selectPlayerClusterTarget`). Colossus applies `resolveBodyClamped` / `checkDashWallBounds`, Overlord clamps to `this.arena.playable` (`world.ts:2409`), and Overlord retains `pendingBacklashes` pylon backlash queueing (`boss-overlord.ts:2092`).

---

## Verdict: FAIL

Four `BLOCKER` and three `MAJOR` integration/clarity defects break boss telegraph visibility, Overlord sub-entities (`weakPoints`, `pylons`, `laserLanes`), Colossus Iron Drone rendering and knockback, gadget-vs-boss damage, crit/stagger/threat mechanics, and boss HP scaling in live `BansungWorld` sessions.
