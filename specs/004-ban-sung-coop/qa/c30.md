# QA Audit Report — Slice c30: End-to-End Spec & Gameplay Parity

## Scope

End-to-end cross-check of specification requirements and runtime symbol/data parity across `shared/`, `server/`, `client/`, and release verification gates for slice `c30` (`004-ban-sung-coop`):
- **Soul Knight + Miragine visuals & assets**: hero/mob/pet rendering, shadows, evolved weapon accents (`Mk.I..IV`), VFX, HUD/hotbar, audio cues, and static asset verification.
- **Co-op Bosses**: Sand Beholder, Dread Colossus, and Overlord Core phases, telegraphs, stuns/DR windows, adds, co-op HP scaling, and boss slot rotation.
- **Expanded `4224x2688` Map**: `88x56` tiles at `48px`, 3 biomes, 12 enemy gates, 5 pedestals, spatial culling, `512px` terrain chunks, and `11:7` (`154x98`) minimap.
- **XP Farm + 3-Card Gacha + Gun Evolution**: `xp_orb_s/m/l`, 40% co-op XP resonance, `[1/2/3]` Gacha picks, 6 `skill_*` categories, `weapon_upgrade` scaling, and bot auto-pick.
- **20-Min Survival Loop**: `survival` default vs `waves` classic compatibility, `4x5m` stage progression (`swarm_light -> heavy_brute -> boss_hunt -> evolved_horde`), seamless `cycle + 1` wrap, lobby/rules/HUD/post-game parity.
- **Release Gates**: `verify-assets.mjs`, `server/test/bansung-*.test.cjs`, `npm run build`, and `npm --prefix server test` (zero production source or asset files modified).

## Cross-Check Findings

### [BLOCKER]

1. **Co-op pets never serialized in `BansungFrame` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts` -> `client/src/bansung/render.ts`)**
   - `BansungWorld.petTuples()` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:1225-1227`) encodes `this.players.map((p) => serializePetTuple(p.pet))`, and `syncFramePetsIfPresent()` (`client/src/bansung/render.ts:3233-3244`) consumes `rawFrame?.pets`.
   - `BansungWorld.frameFor()` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:4031-4047`) omits any `pets` key (`{ t, ms, w, wp, rem, p, e, b, pr, pk, fx, bs, srv, me, full }`), so server pet mechanics run invisibly with zero live pet rendering on the client.

### [MAJOR]

2. **Bot Gacha priority comparison is inverted (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:503-511, 3015-3018`)**
   - `BOT_GACHA_CATEGORY_PRIORITY` (`L503-511`) assigns lowest rank numbers to top priorities (`weapon_upgrade: 0, skill_damage: 1, skill_hp: 2, skill_firerate: 3, skill_multishot: 4, skill_crit: 5, skill_mobility: 6`).
   - `pickBestGachaIdxForBot()` (`L3015-3018`) uses `if (prio > bestPrio || (prio === bestPrio && rarity > bestRarity))`, systematically rejecting `weapon_upgrade` (`0`) and `skill_damage` (`1`) in favor of `skill_mobility` (`6`) / `skill_crit` (`5`) and starving AI teammates of Mk.II–IV gun evolutions.

3. **Survival Gacha `critDmgMult` & firearm `energyCostMult` silently dropped (`shared/`, `pickups.ts`, `world.ts`, `weapons.ts`)**
   - `BANSUNG_GACHA_SKILLS` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2754-2805`) grants crit damage (`+15%..+60%` `secondaryValue` on `skill_crit`) and energy cost discount (`0..0.12` on `skill_multishot`), stored in `g.gachaEnergyCostMult` (`server/src/game/bansung/pickups.ts:884`) and `g.gachaCritDmgMult` (`pickups.ts:895`).
   - `BansungWorld.syncWeaponAndHeroState()` (`world.ts:2841-2850`) copies 7 fields into `p.weaponState.gachaMods` (`BansungHeroGachaMods` in `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:245-253`, which omits both fields), dropping `critDmgMult` completely; `getEffectiveEnergyCost()` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:843`) only reads `hero.mods?.energyCostMul`, so `gachaEnergyCostMult` never reduces firearm energy usage (`world.ts:3439` only forwards it to gadgets).

4. **Hotbar slot compaction, reload sweet-spot scale, HUD status field & ally bar scale mismatches (`weapons.ts`, `world.ts`, `HotbarShop.tsx`, `HudBars.tsx`)**
   - **Slot index drift**: `buildWeaponSlotsSnapshot()` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:1052-1067`) compacts out falsy slots (`if (!s) continue`), while `buildPrivateFor()` (`world.ts:3903-3904`) builds `weaponTiers` across uncompacted indices (`p.weaponState.slots.map((s) => (s ? s.tier : 1))`). `HotbarShop.tsx:1441, 2437-2464` indexes `me.slots[me.slotIdx]` and `me.slots[3]` (`SupplyHotbarSlot`), misaligning active weapon state and supply slot whenever any slot is empty.
   - **Active reload 100x mismatch**: `world.ts:3925-3928` sends `sweetSpot` in `0..100` (`Math.round(reload.sweetMinU * 100)`, e.g. `48..64`), while `ActiveReloadStrip` (`client/src/components/bansung/HotbarShop.tsx:1449-1466`) compares against `0..1` (`u`), pushing the sweet-spot zone off-bar (`left: 4800%`).
   - **HUD `FX_STATUS` index bug**: `HudBars.tsx:2599-2610` reads `const statusKind = ev.d;` instead of `ev.b` on `[5, playerIdx, statusKind, val, x, y]` tuples, suppressing `fxLevelUpAt`, `fxReloadPerfectedAt`, and `fxDownedAt` HUD feedback.
   - **Squad bar 10x scale bug**: `SquadStrip` (`HudBars.tsx:1223-1224`) derives `maxHp = Math.max(hero.hp, ally.hp)` and `maxSh = Math.max(hero.shield, ally.shield, 1)` without `* 10` (`HERO_STAT_SCALE = 10` at `world.ts:472`), pinning ally HP/shield bars at `100%` until values drop to `<= 5..7`.

5. **Classic pickups, perks & bot low-shield threshold left unscaled vs `HERO_STAT_SCALE = 10` (`shared/enemies.ts`, `pickups.ts`, `bot.ts`)**
   - Heroes spawn at `hp = heroSpec.hp * 10` (`50..70`) and `shield = heroSpec.shield * 10` (`40..70`) (`world.ts:472, 863-864`), Survival `skill_hp` adds `+25/+45/+75/+120` Max HP (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2580-2625`), and basic enemies deal `8..34` damage (`shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts:147, 179, 212, 245`).
   - Classic pickups/perks remain unscaled (`enemies.ts:1836-1855, 2188-2261`, `pickups.ts:721, 732, 1840-1855`): `hp_orb` (`+2`), `hp_pack` (`+4`), `armor_plate` (`+2`), `vitality_boost` (`+4` Max HP), and `iron_bastion` (`+3` Max Armor) are ~10x too weak; `bot.ts:1691` checks `getPlayerMaxShield(bot) <= 6`, unreachable under `40..70` scaling.

6. **Survival post-game wave summary renders stale `Đợt 0/10` (`director.ts`, `world.ts`, `PostGameModal.tsx`)**
   - `stepSurvival()` (`server/src/game/bansung/director.ts`) never updates `this.wavesCleared`, yet `finalizeResult()` (`world.ts:2805-2808`) still reports `wavesCleared: this.director.wavesCleared` (`0`) and `totalWaves: 10`.
   - `client/src/components/bansung/PostGameModal.tsx:1108-1122, 1231` displays `"Đợt 0/10"` and `"Cả đội đã gục tại Đợt 0"` in Survival mode instead of survival duration, cycle, and stage.

7. **Missing initial arena weapon pedestals & Gacha/Level-Up AV parity gaps (`world.ts`, `render.ts`, `sound.ts`)**
   - `BansungWorld` (`world.ts:811-850`) does not spawn initial weapon pedestals at `arena.pedestals` (`PEDESTAL_ANCHORS`) on match start.
   - `world.ts:2967` emits `clampedIdx` (`0|1|2`) as `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA` `val` (`evt.c`), whereas `render.ts:2960-2970` expects weapon tier `2..4` to color `EVOLVED!` text — miscoloring/suppressing Tier-3/4 evolution bursts while falsely tagging skill picks (`clampedIdx = 1|2`) as `EVOLVED!`.
   - `consumeFrameAudioEvents()` (`client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1838-1854`) `FX_STATUS` switch has no audio handler for `BANSUNG_STATUS_LEVELUP` or `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA`.

### [MINOR]

8. **Survival cycle log 0-based vs HUD 1-based (`world.ts:2584` vs `HudBars.tsx:2153`, `BansungBoard.tsx:906`)**
