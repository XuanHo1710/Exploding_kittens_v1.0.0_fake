## Cross-Check Findings

### [MAJOR] 1. Survival Gacha `energyCostMult` and `critDmgMult` modifiers are dropped before weapon combat
- **Shared & Pickup State**:
  - `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts` defines `skill_multishot` energy-cost reductions (`rare.secondaryValue = 0.05` at L2715, `epic.secondaryValue = 0.08` at L2729, `legendary.secondaryValue = 0.12` at L2743) and `skill_crit` crit-damage bonuses (`common`–`legendary` `secondaryValue = 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.40 / 0.60` at L2759, L2774, L2789, L2804).
  - `server/src/game/bansung/pickups.ts` declares `critDmgMult` and `energyCostMult` on `SurvivalWeaponGachaMods` (L147, L150), accumulates `g.gachaEnergyCostMult = Math.max(0.5, g.gachaEnergyCostMult - enSave)` (L884) and `g.gachaCritDmgMult += critDmgAdd` (L895) in `applySurvivalGachaSkillToHero`, and writes both into `hero.gachaMods` (`syncSurvivalWeaponGachaMods`, L814, L817).
- **Parity Break**:
  - `BansungHeroGachaMods` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:245-253`) omits both `energyCostMult` and `critDmgMult`, keeping only 7 fields (`dmgMult`, `fireRateMult`, `extraMultishot`, `critChanceAdd`, `extraPierce`, `blastRadiusMult`, `reloadSpeedMul`).
  - `World.syncWeaponAndHeroState` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2841-2851`) copies only those 7 fields into `p.weaponState.gachaMods`, discarding `p.gachaMods.energyCostMult` and `p.gachaMods.critDmgMult` every tick. (By contrast, `toGadgetPlayerActor` at `world.ts:3438-3439` does apply `(p.gachaEnergyCostMult ?? 1)` and `this.director.getEnergyCostMult()` to gadgets.)
  - `getEffectiveEnergyCost` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:837-848`) only multiplies `evoEnergyCostMult` and `hero.mods?.energyCostMul`, ignoring `gachaMods.energyCostMult` (and `director.getEnergyCostMult()`).
  - `applyBansungDamage` (`server/src/game/bansung/damage.ts:448-449`) supports `hit.atkCritDmgBonus ?? ctx?.atkCritDmgBonus`, but every weapon hit/projectile in `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts` (L1585, L1625, L1735, L1826, L1995, L2219, L2681, L2804, L3188) passes `critMult: spec.critMult` without `atkCritDmgBonus` or any `gachaMods.critDmgMult` delta.
  - `T08.4` (`server/test/bansung-weapons.test.cjs:1761-1804`) missed this because it asserted `hero.gachaEnergyCostMult < 1` and `hero.gachaCritDmgMult === 1.6` on the pickup object, then tested combat using `pistol` (`energyCost: 0`) with `rng: () => 0.99` (preventing crits).

### [MAJOR] 2. Active-reload `sweetSpot` unit mismatch (`0..100` vs `0..1`) breaks client HUD/canvas bars, and per-weapon `sweetSpot` specs are unused on server
- **Wire Unit Mismatch (`0..100` emitted, `0..1` consumed)**:
  - `BansungPrivate.sweetSpot` (`shared/src/bansung/types.ts:501`) is populated by `World.buildPrivateFor` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3923-3930`) as integer percentages `[Math.round(reload.sweetMinU * 100), Math.round(reload.sweetMaxU * 100)]` (e.g., `[48, 64]`).
  - `client/src/bansung/store.ts` (`normalizePrivateSlice`, L452-495) passes `...raw` through without dividing `sweetSpot` by `100`.
  - All three client UI consumers treat `sweetSpot` as normalized `0..1` ratios:
    - `ActiveReloadBar` (`client/src/components/bansung/HudBars.tsx:1146-1147`, fed `me?.sweetSpot` at L1470) clamps `sweetSpot?.[0]` into `[BANSUNG_RELOAD_LOCKOUT_U, 0.92]` and `sweetSpot?.[1]` into `[minU + 0.04, 0.98]`, pinning `[48, 64]` to `[0.92, 0.98]`.
    - `ActiveReloadStrip` (`client/src/components/bansung/HotbarShop.tsx:1449-1467`) computes `left: ${sweetMin * 100}%` (`4800%`), `width: ${Math.max(2, (sweetMax - sweetMin) * 100)}%` (`1600%`), and evaluates `u >= sweetMin && u <= sweetMax` (`u` in `0..1` vs `48..64`), so `inSweet` is always `false`.
    - Overhead world reload bar (`client/src/bansung/render.ts:3183-3189`) computes `sx = bx + Math.round(spot[0] * bw)` (`bx + 1824px` on a `38px` bar) and `sw = Math.round((spot[1] - spot[0]) * bw)`, drawing the green sweet-spot off-bar.
  - Meanwhile `server/src/game/bansung/bot.ts:1514-1515` expects `0..100` (`bot.sweetSpot[0] / 100`), though `World.toBotPlayerView` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3488-3540`) omits both `reloadTotalMs` and `sweetSpot`, preventing bots from running the `u` check at `bot.ts:1510`.
- **Unused Per-Weapon `sweetSpot` Specs**:
  - `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts` defines per-weapon windows on `pistol: [0.45, 0.68]` (L869), `rifle: [0.50, 0.70]` (L916), `smg: [0.45, 0.65]` (L963), `shotgun: [0.55, 0.75]` (L1010), `bazooka: [0.50, 0.70]` (L1277), and `railgun: [0.50, 0.66]` (L1533), matching `d26-weapons-kinetic.md`.
  - `getEffectiveReloadParams` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:817-835`) ignores `spec.sweetSpot` and uses `[BANSUNG_RELOAD_SWEET_MIN, BANSUNG_RELOAD_SWEET_MAX]` (`[0.48, 0.64]`), or `BANSUNG_QUICK_HANDS_SWEET_SPOT` (`[0.44, 0.68]`) when `quickHands` is active.

### [MINOR] 3. Gun Evolution `buildWeaponUpgradeCard` & `getWeaponEvolutionStats` display/rounding discrepancies vs weapon categories
- **Melee Evolution Cards & `totalProjectiles`**: `getWeaponEvolutionStats` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2421-2423,2450`) sets `baseProj = 0` for `category === 'melee'` so `extraProjectiles` (`1..3`) yields `totalProjectiles: 1..3`, and `buildWeaponUpgradeCard` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2943-2955`) unconditionally formats `+{dmgPct}% DMG, +{rofPct}% ROF, +{extraProjDelta} Proj, -{enPct}% Energy` for all weapons — including melee (`crescent_blade` / `reaper_scythe` / `shock_baton`) and `0`-energy weapons (`pistol`, `crescent_blade`). In runtime, `startMeleeSwing` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:2029-2090`) does not spawn extra projectiles or swings from `evo.extraProjectiles`, instead scaling `evo.dmgMult`, `evo.fireRateMult`, `evo.energyCostMult`, `evo.meleeReachMult`, and `evo.meleeArcBonusDeg`.
- **1-Energy Quantization & `ceil` vs `round` Asymmetry**: `rifle` has `energyCost: 1` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:912`); both `getWeaponEvolutionStats` (`L2414`) and `getEffectiveEnergyCost` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:847`) clamp non-zero costs to `>= 1`, so `rifle` stays at `1` energy through Mk.IV despite upgrade cards advertising `-8%` / `-11%` / `-15% Energy`. In addition, `getEffectiveEnergyCost` uses `Math.ceil` when `evoEnergyCostMult === 1` (`L844-845`) vs `Math.round` when `evoEnergyCostMult !== 1` (`L847`), causing an uneven threshold jump when perks combine with Mk.I vs Mk.II+, and omits `director.getEnergyCostMult()` (`server/src/game/bansung/director.ts:1229-1236`).
- **`extraPierce` Tier Plateau**: `BANSUNG_WEAPON_TIERS` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2308,2329,2350`) keeps `extraPierce: 1` flat across Mk.II and Mk.III before stepping to `2` at Mk.IV (consistent between shared and server, noted as a tier-table quirk).

### [MINOR] 4. `skill_multishot.epic` card description omits its `-8% Energy Cost` secondary effect
- `BANSUNG_GACHA_SKILL_MAP.skill_multishot.epic` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2719-2731`) sets `secondaryValue: 0.08` (consumed by `pickups.ts:881-885`), matching `rare` (`0.05`, `desc: '+1 Extra Projectile & -5% Energy Cost'`, L2710-2715) and `legendary` (`0.12`, `desc: '+3 Extra Projectiles & -12% Energy Cost'`, L2738-2743), but `epic`'s `desc` (`'+2 Extra Projectiles per shot'`, L2724) and `vnDesc` (`'+2 Tia đạn / Viên bắn mỗi phát'`, L2725) fail to mention `-8% Energy Cost`.
