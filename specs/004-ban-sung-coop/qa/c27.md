## Cross-Check Findings

### 1. `PostGameModal.tsx` (and `BansungResult` / `world.ts`) lacks Survival 20-Min mode adaptation `[severity: MAJOR]`
- **Evidence**:
  - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2800-2812` (`finalizeResult`) populates `BansungResult` (`shared/src/bansung/types.ts:266-280`) only with wave counters (`wavesCleared`, `totalWaves`, `wave`) and passes no Survival fields (`mode`, `cycle`, `stageId`, `level`).
  - `client/src/components/bansung/PostGameModal.tsx:1101-1123` never checks `resolveBansungGameMode(cfg)` or `hud.survival?.mode` (unlike `BansungBoard.tsx:905-914`, which adapts the Tab scoreboard header to Survival).
  - `PostGameModal.tsx:1108-1109, 1118-1122, 1230-1233` unconditionally display `Đợt {wavesCleared}/{totalWaves}` (e.g., `Đợt 0/10` / `Đợt 1/10`) in `.bs-end-meta`, and fall back to ``Đã hoàn thành ${wavesCleared}/${totalWaves} đợt quái!`` / ``Cả đội đã gục tại Đợt ${wavesCleared}.`` instead of Survival Cycle (`Vòng`), Stage (`0–5p / 5–10p / 10–15p / 15–20p`), or Hero Level (`LV`).

### 2. Survival Level-Up badge flash broken by `FX_STATUS` field mismatch in `HudBars.tsx` `[severity: MAJOR]`
- **Evidence**:
  - `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:372-373, 392-398` defines `FX_STATUS = 5` tuple layout `[5, playerIdx, statusKind, val, x, y]` with `BANSUNG_STATUS_RELOAD_PERFECT = 2`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_DOWNED = 3`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_LEVELUP = 7`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA = 8`.
  - `client/src/bansung/store.ts:613-625` (`decodeFxArray`) maps `a = playerIdx`, `b = statusKind`, `c = val`, `d = x`, `e = y`.
  - `client/src/components/bansung/HudBars.tsx:2595-2614` (mounted by `BansungBoard.tsx:804`) reads `const statusKind = ev.d;` at `L2600` (world `x` coordinate) instead of `ev.b`.
  - Because `TopLeftHud` (`HudBars.tsx:1333-1339, 1405-1409`) drives `.bs-lv-badge--flash` (`bansung.css:953-955`) exclusively from `fxLevelUpAt` (`HudBars.tsx:2641`), Survival XP level-ups and Gacha picks never trigger the level-up pulse animation (and `FX_STATUS`-based `RELOAD_PERFECT` / `DOWNED` triggers in `HudBars` also misfire).

### 3. Survival level-up/Gacha SFX missing & stage cue mount/reset nuances `[severity: MINOR]`
- **Evidence**:
  - `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1824-1855` (`consumeFrameAudioEvents`) correctly reads `const kind = evt.b;` and handles `BANSUNG_STATUS_DASH`..`BANSUNG_STATUS_CRATE` (`0..6`), but has no branch for `BANSUNG_STATUS_LEVELUP` (`7`) or `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA` (`8`), and `BansungBoard.tsx` plays no SFX for level-up/Gacha either.
  - `client/src/components/BansungBoard.tsx:331-346` fires `'boss_roar'` on transition to `'boss_hunt'` and `'wave_start'` (`pitchMul: 1.12`) on `'evolved_horde'`, but its `!lastSurvivalStageRef.current` guard (`L334-337`) skips the stinger if a player joins/reconnects mid-match already inside `boss_hunt` or `evolved_horde` (though `'boss'` BGM still activates via `L315-328`).
  - `BansungBoard.tsx:241-250` resets `lastSurvivalStageRef.current = ''` on `sessionKey` change, while `lastBgmKeyRef.current` is not explicitly cleared (normally masked on end->restart because `'victory'|'defeat'` differs from `'combat'`).

### 4. Tab Live Scoreboard displays static `hero.hp` max and `-` for teammate combat stats `[severity: MINOR]`
- **Evidence**:
  - `client/src/components/BansungBoard.tsx:990-996` renders non-local HP using `{snapP ? `${Math.max(0, Math.round(snapP.hp))}/${isMe && me ? me.maxHp : hero.hp}` : '-'}` and prints `'-'` for teammates' `Hạ`, `ST`, `Hồi sinh`, and `Xu`.
  - In Survival mode, teammates gaining max HP from Gacha `skill_hp` cards render `hp > hero.hp` (e.g. `160/100`), while only local stats exist in `BansungPrivate`. (Weapon tier badge `.bs-scoreboard-tier` at `L981-988` correctly reads `snapP.weaponTier`.)

### 5. Narrow-viewport Survival HUD overlap & loading-pill / crosshair backdrop collisions `[severity: MINOR]`
- **Evidence**:
  - `client/src/bansung/bansung.css:1104-1113` (`@media (max-width: 760px), (pointer: coarse)`) sizes `.bs-game .bs-survival-strip` at `width: min(320px, 52vw); top: max(8px, env(safe-area-inset-top));`. Together with `HudBars.tsx:919-928` (`.bs-hud-tl` at `left: 8px`, `.bs-hud-tr` minimap `114px` at `right: 8px`), narrow phones (`<= 560px` wide) horizontally overlap the centered Survival strip with the left hero card and right minimap.
  - `bansung.css:1110-1112` overrides `.bs-survival-strip--under-boss` to `+56px` while `HudBars.tsx:925` uses `64px` (safe cascade because `bansung.css` is unlayered, unlike `@layer bs-hud-default`, though inconsistent).
  - `bansung.css:103-108` places `.bs-loading-pill` at `top: calc(max(12px, env(safe-area-inset-top)) + 64px); z-index: 22`, sharing the exact vertical band (`+64px`) of `.bs-game .bs-survival-strip--under-boss` (`bansung.css:967-969`) while Tier-1 sprites hydrate (`BansungBoard.tsx:676-683`).
  - `BansungBoard.tsx:213` `uiBlockRef.current = ended || guideOpen || leaveOpen` excludes `hasDraftOverlay`, so `showCrosshair` (`L549`) stays drawn behind `.bs-perk-backdrop` (`HotbarShop.tsx:657-661`, `z-index: 25`), although `.bs-perk-backdrop` absorbs pointer clicks and `.bs-game.bs-cursor-none .bs-canvas` (`bansung.css:48-50`) leaves the system pointer visible on the Gacha/Perk modal.

## Verdict: FAIL

**Reason**: Two `MAJOR` cross-check defects prevent PASS: (1) `PostGameModal.tsx` (and `BansungResult` / `world.ts`) displays Wave-mode `Đợt 0/10` / wave counts instead of Survival 20-min Cycle/Stage/Level summary, and (2) `HudBars.tsx:2600` reads `ev.d` instead of `ev.b` for `FX_STATUS`, breaking Survival `.bs-lv-badge--flash` level-up HUD feedback rendered under `BansungBoard.tsx`.
