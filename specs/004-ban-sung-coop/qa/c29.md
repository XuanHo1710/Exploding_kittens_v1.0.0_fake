## Cross-Check Findings

1. **[MAJOR] Flaky CPU-contention wall-clock budget and vacuous `stepTimeEmaMs` assertion in `2a` (`server/test/bansung-net-security.test.cjs:521-563`)**
   - Lines `560-561` enforce `elapsedMs < 1000` (`avgMs < 8.5`) for 120 `w.step()` ticks plus 480 `w.frameFor()` calls, even though the 30Hz real-time budget is `33.33ms/tick` (`4000ms` total). Under `npm --prefix server test` parallel execution across 24 `*.test.cjs` files, CPU contention caused `task-37` to reach `1539.12ms` (`12.83ms/tick`) and fail the build gate.
   - Line `562` asserts `Number.isFinite(w.stepTimeEmaMs) && w.stepTimeEmaMs < 15`, which is **vacuous**: `BansungWorld.stepTimeEmaMs` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:784`) is initialized to `0` and updated only inside `pump()` (`world.ts:1098-1100`), never inside `step()`, so it stays `0` throughout test `2a`.

2. **[MAJOR] Silent no-op `sources.json` vs `LICENSES.md` license-parity check (`server/test/bansung-assets.test.cjs:355-363`)**
   - `database-ban-sung/sources.json:80` defines `spritePacks` (and `audioPacks`) as JSON arrays, not maps keyed by `sourceId`.
   - `bansung-assets.test.cjs:356-363` loops `for (const [id, spec] of Object.entries(group))` and guards `if (packIds.has(id)) { assert.equal(packIds.get(id), spec.license); }`. Because `id` evaluates to `"0"`, `"1"`, `"2"`, etc., `packIds.has(id)` is always `false`, silently skipping license-parity verification between `sources.json` and `LICENSES.md`.

3. **[MAJOR] Survival Gacha combat/world integration gaps mask runtime wiring & priority bugs**
   - **Silent drop of Gacha combat mods (`critDmgMult`, `energyCostMult`)**: `server/test/bansung-weapons.test.cjs:1761-1807` (`T08.4`) checks `hero.gachaCritDmgMult` (`1772`) and `hero.gachaEnergyCostMult` (`1766`) only on `pickups.ts` state, then passes a manually patched `{ ...hero.gachaMods, reloadSpeedMul: hero.gachaReloadSpeedMult }` (`1795-1798`) to `weapons.js` without testing actual crit damage or weapon energy deduction — leaving `BansungHeroGachaMods` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:245-253`) and `BansungWorld.syncWeaponAndHeroState()` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2841-2851`) silently dropping `critDmgMult` and `energyCostMult` untested.
