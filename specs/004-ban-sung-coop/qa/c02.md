## Cross-Check Findings

1. **[MAJOR] Sparse (`0..4`) vs. compacted (`0..N-1`) `slots` / `slotIdx` / `weaponTiers` protocol desync in `buildPrivateFor` & `store.ts`**
   - `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:630-651` (`createHeroWeaponState`) allocates `p.weaponState.slots` as a **sparse 5-slot array**: `[sidearmWeapon (0), starterWeapon (1), null (2), null (3), 'crescent_blade' (4)]`.
   - Server slot selectors (`p.weaponState.activeSlot` -> `buildPrivateFor` `slotIdx` at `world.ts:3917`), `weaponTiers` (`world.ts:3904`, always length `5`), and Gacha evolution cards (`openNextGachaChoicesForHero` at `world.ts:2892-2903` setting `card.slotIdx` `0..4`) all index into this sparse `0..4` layout.
   - However, `buildWeaponSlotsSnapshot` (`server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:1052-1068`) executes `if (!s) continue;`, emitting `BansungPrivate.slots` as a **compacted** array (initially length `3`: `[sidearm, starter, crescent_blade]`, placing `crescent_blade` at index `2` instead of `4`).
   - Additionally, `buildPrivateFor` (`world.ts:3953`) omits `weaponTiers` whenever `!isInitial && !hasUpgradedWeapon`. In `client/src/bansung/store.ts:478-482`, `normalizePrivateSlice` falls back to `raw.slots.map(...)` when `raw.weaponTiers` is absent, producing a compacted length-`3` `me.weaponTiers` on ticks `>= 2` pre-upgrade (vs length-`5` on initial/post-upgrade frames).
   - Runtime breakage across modules:
     - When slot `4` (`crescent_blade`) is active (`slotIdx === 4`), `p.slots[p.slotIdx]` / `me.slots[me.slotIdx]` is `undefined` unless slots `2` and `3` are both filled — silently disabling server melee projectile destruction (`world.ts:1921-1924`), mis-indexing `interactPedestal` (`world.ts:1399` -> `pickups.ts:1786-1804`, reading `undefined` and consuming the pedestal instead of swapping), breaking `toBotPlayerView` (`world.ts:3489`), `HotbarShop.tsx:1441-1445,2437-2464` (renders `crescent_blade` in hotbar slot `2` while `slotIdx === 4` highlights none), `HudBars.tsx:1328`, and `input.ts:283` (`resolveAutoAimRange` falls back to default).
     - `server/test/bansung-net-security.test.cjs:1537` (`assert.equal(postEvoFrame.me.slots[upgradedSlot].tier, 2)`) only passes when the test seed picks `upgradedSlot` in `{0, 1}`; rolling `slotIdx === 4` (`crescent_blade`) would dereference `undefined`.

2. **[MAJOR] `BansungPrivate.sweetSpot` unit scale mismatch (`0..100` packed by server vs `0..1` consumed by client)**
   - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3925-3928` (`buildPrivateFor`) packs `sweetSpot` as integer percentages in `[0..100]`: `[Math.round(reload.sweetMinU * 100), Math.round(reload.sweetMaxU * 100)]` (e.g. `[48, 64]`; `server/src/game/bansung/bot.ts:1514-1515` correspondingly divides `bot.sweetSpot[0/1] / 100`).
   - `client/src/bansung/store.ts:485` (`normalizePrivateSlice`) spreads `...raw` without scaling `sweetSpot` back into `0..1`.
   - Both client reload consumers expect `[0..1]` fractions matching `BANSUNG_RELOAD_SWEET_MIN = 0.48` / `BANSUNG_RELOAD_SWEET_MAX = 0.64` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:43-45`):
     - `client/src/components/bansung/HotbarShop.tsx:1448-1451,1464-1465` compares normalized `u` (`0..1`) against `sweetMin..sweetMax` (`48..64`, so `inSweet` never activates) and computes `left: ${sweetMin * 100}%` (`4800%`) / `width: ${(sweetMax - sweetMin) * 100}%` (`1600%`).
     - `client/src/bansung/render.ts:3183-3188` computes `sx = bx + Math.round(spot[0] * bw)` (`bw = 38`), drawing the overhead reload sweet-spot segment `+1824px` off the bar.

3. **[MINOR] `BANSUNG_PFLAG_LEFT` bit conflated with match-resignation `p.left` in `packAllPlayers`**
   - `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:334` defines `BANSUNG_PFLAG_LEFT = 1` (alongside `BANSUNG_EFLAG_LEFT = 1`), and `client/src/bansung/store.ts:1879` decodes `(src.flags & BANSUNG_PFLAG_LEFT) !== 0` as sprite `facingLeft` orientation.
   - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3858,3862` sets `BANSUNG_PFLAG_LEFT` first on `p.aimCos < 0` and then again on `if (p.left) flags |= BANSUNG_PFLAG_LEFT;` (`p.left` set by `world.ts:1113` `resign()`), conflating resigned/disconnected state with left-facing direction without a dedicated `LEFT` status flag.

4. **[MINOR] `gachaPending` semantics (`total remaining` in `world.ts`/`store.ts` vs `+N hộp chờ` extra-queue label in UI)**
   - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2883,2912,2975` computes `p.gachaPending = p.gachaQueue + (p.gachaChoices ? 1 : 0)` (so a single unqueued level-up modal sets `gachaPending = 1`), and `client/src/bansung/store.ts:464-471` mirrors this by defaulting omitted `gachaPending` to `1` when `gachaChoices.length > 0`.
   - `client/src/components/bansung/HotbarShop.tsx:2163-2167` renders `+{gachaPending} hộp chờ` inside `GachaDraftModal` whenever `gachaPending > 0`, displaying `+1 hộp chờ` even when `gachaQueue === 0` (`HudBars.tsx:1434-1441` displays `+{gachaPending} GACHA`).

5. **[MINOR] `BANSUNG_PICKUP_IDS` / `BANSUNG_PICKUP_INDEX` (`0..6`) diverges from runtime `pk` `kindIdx` (`0..10`)**
   - `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:108,137,328` defines `BANSUNG_PICKUP_IDS` (`['hp', 'energy', 'armor', 'coin', 'weapon', 'ammo', 'xp']`) and `BANSUNG_PICKUP_INDEX` (`0..6`), whereas `server/src/game/bansung/pickups.ts:2083` packs `BANSUNG_PICKUPS[p.kind].kindIdx` (`0..10` in `shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts:2187-2353`, where `xp_orb_s/m/l` occupy `8..10`).
   - Runtime decoding (`store.ts:584-595`) preserves raw `kindIdx` and `render.ts:274-286` prioritizes `BANSUNG_PICKUPS` over `BANSUNG_PICKUP_IDS`, so rendering succeeds, leaving `BANSUNG_PICKUP_INDEX` unused (0 callers outside `types.ts`) and unaligned with the wire `pickupKindIdx` enum.

## Verified Invariants

- **12-Field Player Tuple (`BANSUNG_PLAYER_FIELDS = 12`)**:
  - `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:321,526`, `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3845-3882` (`packAllPlayers`), and `client/src/bansung/store.ts:497-524,1909` (`decodePlayers`, `upsertPlayer`) agree 1:1 on tuple length `12` and field order `[idx, x, y, aimQ, hp, shield, state, flags, heroIdx, weaponIdx, revivePct, weaponTier]`, clamping `weaponTier` (`+11`) to `[1, 4]` and mapping `state` `0..6` identically (`types.ts:115-122`, `world.ts:3849-3855`).
- **Remaining Entity & Delta Strides (`5 / 8 / 6 / 5 / 5 / 6`)**:
  - `decodeObstacles` (stride `5`, `store.ts:598-611`), `decodeEnemies` (stride `8`, `store.ts:526-545` <-> `world.ts:3996`), `decodeBullets` (stride `6`, `store.ts:547-568` <-> `weapons.ts:1365-1394` / `world.ts:4004`), `decodeProps` (stride `5`, `store.ts:570-582` <-> `world.ts:4011-4014`), `decodePickups` (stride `5`, `store.ts:584-596` <-> `pickups.ts:2077-2090`), and `decodeFxArray` (stride `6`, `store.ts:613-625` <-> `world.ts:4019-4025`) all match `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:319-331` and safely retain delta-omitted `pr`/`pk` across non-`full` frames (`store.ts:1123-1135`).
- **Survival Clock & Gacha Field Alignment**:
  - `BANSUNG_SURVIVAL_STAGE_IDS` (`types.ts:413-418`) matches `BANSUNG_SURVIVAL_STAGES` (`enemies.ts:1518-1587`) and `director.ts:986-1001` (`getSurvivalClock`). `world.ts:4029,4044` attaches `frame.srv` exclusively when `resolveBansungGameMode(this.config) === 'survival'`, and `store.ts:1154` assigns `frame.srv ?? null`.
  - When `buildPrivateFor` omits optional `BansungPrivate` fields on delta ticks (`xp === 0`, `level === 1`, empty `gachaChoices`/`skillStacks`, stripped `evolvedName`), `normalizePrivateSlice` (`store.ts:452-495`) restores defaults (`level=1`, `xp=0`, `xpToNext=getXpToNextLevel(level)`, `gachaChoices=[]`, `gachaRemMs=0`, `skillStacks={}`), and `HotbarShop.tsx:1445` falls back to `getWeaponEvolutionStats` for `evolvedName`.
- **`normalizeBansungConfig` & `resolveBansungGameMode` Parity**:
  - `normalizeBansungConfig` (`types.ts:170-197`) clamps `bots` cleanly to `[0, min(BANSUNG_MAX_BOTS, BANSUNG_MAX_PLAYERS - safeHumans)]`, falls back invalid enums to `DEFAULT_BANSUNG_CONFIG`, omits optional `gameMode`/`endless` only when `undefined`, and is invoked consistently across `security.ts:345`, `game.ts:296,366,1807`, and `world.ts:819`.
  - `resolveBansungGameMode` (`types.ts:199-201`) defaults omitted `gameMode` to `'survival'` identically across `world.ts:839,4029`, `Lobby.tsx:161-162`, and `HudBars.tsx:183`.

---

## Verdict: FAIL

Two `MAJOR` protocol bugs in `buildPrivateFor` / `normalizePrivateSlice` require remediation: (1) compacted `slots` vs sparse `0..4` `slotIdx`/`weaponTiers` indexing (plus omitted `weaponTiers` fallback collapsing `me.weaponTiers` length to `3`), and (2) `sweetSpot` `[0..100]` wire scale mismatch against `[0..1]` client reload consumers (`HotbarShop.tsx`, `render.ts`). Three additional `MINOR` semantic discrepancies (`BANSUNG_PFLAG_LEFT`, `gachaPending` UI copy, unused `BANSUNG_PICKUP_INDEX`) are documented above.
