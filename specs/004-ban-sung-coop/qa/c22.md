## Cross-Check Findings

### 1. `[BLOCKER]` `FX_MUZZLE` `evt.a >= 1000` Guard Misroutes All Player Weapon Shots as Turret Audio
- **Locations:**
  - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:857` (`const actorId = 1000 + idx;`)
  - `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:1638-1644, 1766-1772, 1857-1863, 2019-2025, 2074-2080` (`ctx.fx?.push(FX_MUZZLE, hero.actorId, spec.idx, ...)`)
  - `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:91, 967, 1756-1762` (`INITIAL_DEPLOYABLE_ID = 1000`, `dep.id` starting at `1000` -> `[FX_MUZZLE, dep.id, projCfg.kindId, ...]`)
  - `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:1819` (`shock_baton` `idx: 14`)
  - `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1742-1759` (`consumeFrameAudioEvents`, `FX_MUZZLE` branch)
- **Mismatch:** Both player actors (`hero.actorId = 1000 + idx`, i.e., `1000..1003`) and deployable turrets (`dep.id >= 1000`) use `evt.a >= 1000`. Because `sound.ts:1744-1748` intercepts every `FX_MUZZLE` event with `if (evt.a >= 1000)`:
  1. **Every player weapon shot** (both local and co-op allies across kinetic, explosive, energy, railgun, and melee swings) is misclassified as a turret shot and plays `'turret_shot'`, completely bypassing `getWeaponByIdx(evt.b)` (`sound.ts:1757-1758`), weapon-specific `wpn.sfxFire`, and `sourceId: evt.a`.
  2. Players swinging `shock_baton` (`spec.idx === 14`) accidentally match `evt.b === 14` (meant for `turret_thunder` projectile kind `14`) and play `'fx_thunder_arc'` instead of `'sfx_melee_baton'`.
  3. Lines `1749-1758` (`echoLocal` + per-weapon lookup) are unreachable dead code for player shots. *(Cross-module callers `render.ts:2788` and `vfx.ts:3049` fail on the exact same `>= 1000` check.)*

### 2. `[MAJOR]` Unconditional `isLocalActor` Echo Suppression with Zero Local Prediction Callers Silences Local Dash & Reload SFX
- **Locations:**
  - `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1722-1726` (`markLocalPredictedAction`)
  - `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1749-1755, 1827-1837` (`consumeFrameAudioEvents` echo checks)
  - `client/src/bansung/render.ts:2843` (`consumeFrameAudioEvents(events, opts?.myPlayerId, { localPlayerIdx: store.myIdx })`)
  - `server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:331-338` (`[FX_STATUS, input.playerIdx, BANSUNG_STATUS_DASH, ...]`)
  - `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:890-898, 938-946` (`[FX_STATUS, hero.idx, BANSUNG_STATUS_RELOAD_START | BANSUNG_STATUS_RELOAD_PERFECT, ...]`)
- **Mismatch:** `consumeFrameAudioEvents` suppresses (`break`) `BANSUNG_STATUS_DASH` (`0`), `BANSUNG_STATUS_RELOAD_START` (`1`), and `BANSUNG_STATUS_RELOAD_PERFECT` (`2`) whenever `isLocalActor(evt.a, localPlayerId, opts)` is true (`sound.ts:1833`), assuming the client already triggered local predicted audio. However, a search across all of `client/src/` shows **zero** callers of `markLocalPredictedAction` and **no** local `playBansungSfx` triggers in `input.ts`, `store.ts`, `motion.ts`, `anim.ts` (including `anim.ts:873` `stepSfx`), or `BansungBoard.tsx` for shooting, dashing, or reloading.
- **Impact:** The local player never hears their own dash (`'dash'`), reload start (`'reload_start'`), or perfect active reload (`'reload_active_hit'`) cues — only teammates' actions emit audio. Furthermore, once Finding 1 (`evt.a >= 1000`) is fixed, `sound.ts:1750` will immediately mute local player gunfire for the exact same reason unless local shot prediction/SFX is wired or unconditional `isLocalActor` suppression is removed.

### 3. `[MAJOR]` Server `FX_STATUS` Code Collisions Fire Spurious `perk_pick` Audio on Overlord Boss & Drop Level-Up / Gacha Audio
- **Locations:**
  - `shared/src/bansung/types.ts:395-398` (`BANSUNG_STATUS_PERK = 5`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_CRATE = 6`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_LEVELUP = 7`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA = 8`)

  - `server/src/game/bansung/boss-colossus.ts:151-152, 624, 745, 1392` (`STATUS_BOSS_OVERHEAT = 7`, `STATUS_SHIELD_UP = 8`)
  - `server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:2872-2878, 2963-2970` (`BANSUNG_STATUS_LEVELUP = 7`, `BANSUNG_STATUS_GACHA = 8`)
  - `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1824-1855` (`FX_STATUS` switch)
- **Mismatch:**
  1. `boss-overlord.ts:860` emits `FX_STATUS` with `a = 0` and `b = 5` on `weakpoint_matrix` entry. In `sound.ts:1849-1850`, `kind === BANSUNG_STATUS_PERK` (`5`) triggers `playBansungSfx('perk_pick', { local: isLocalActor(evt.a, ...), ... })`, playing a loud non-spatial perk-pickup stinger (for player index `0`) during a boss phase transition.

Meanwhile `boss-colossus.ts:624, 745, 1392` pushes boss status codes `7` and `8` (`STATUS_BOSS_OVERHEAT`, `STATUS_SHIELD_UP`), colliding with the shared player `LEVELUP`/`GACHA` enum values.

### 5. `[MINOR]` `setDeckGain` Missing `cancelScheduledValues(now)` During Rapid BGM Crossfades
- **Location:** `client/src/bansung/sound.ts:1939-1953, 2024, 2039-2041`
- **Detail:** `setDeckGain` calls `deck.gainNode.gain.setValueAtTime(deck.gainNode.gain.value, now)` and `safeParamRamp(...)` without calling `deck.gainNode.gain.cancelScheduledValues(now)`. If `setBansungBgm` transitions faster than the active fade (`1.2s` combat fade interrupted at `0.4s` by a `0.45s` boss crossfade), the previously scheduled `linearRampToValueAtTime` endpoint at `t0 + 1.2s` remains active on the deck's `AudioParam` timeline after the `0.45s` ramp completes, briefly pulling `oldDeck` gain upward before `bgmFadeStopTimer` (`fade + 60ms`, `sound.ts:2027-2036`) pauses it—or distorting `newDeck`'s fade-in if the same deck is reused within the previous fade window.

### 6. `[NONE]` Informational Notes on Sprite Cache Scope & Biome Preload Timing
- **Locations:** `client/src/bansung/sprites.ts:125, 642-643, 696, 747`
- **Observation:**

Switching `biome` after manifest hydration properly enqueues full biome keyword clips at high priority.

## Verdict: **FAIL**
**Summary:** Sprite loading, PNG/manifest alignment, LRU tint/fallback caches, spatial attenuation, 8-voice pooling, and mute/BGM lifecycle wiring are solid, and `client` compiles cleanly with `8/8` `bansung-assets` tests passing. However, audio playback over the wire FX stream has **1 BLOCKER** (`hero.actorId >= 1000` collides with turret IDs so every player weapon shot routes to `'turret_shot'`/`'fx_thunder_arc'` instead of `wpn.sfxFire`) and **2 MAJOR** defects (missing local prediction callers combined with unconditional `isLocalActor` suppression muting local dash/reload/active-reload SFX; Overlord `FX_STATUS` `5` triggering `perk_pick` stingers + dropped Level-Up/Gacha SFX and Colossus status code collision), plus **2 MINOR** findings (`FX_BEAM` Shock Baton playing `'boss_laser'` + ducking BGM, and missing `cancelScheduledValues` in `setDeckGain`).
