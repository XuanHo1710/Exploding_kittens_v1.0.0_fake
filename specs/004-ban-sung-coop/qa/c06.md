## Cross-Check Findings

### [MAJOR] 1. Shallow-copy combat adapters in `world.ts` silently drop gadget damage/stun on Colossus/Overlord/drones, Overlord EMP stuns on players, and Sand Beholder stagger/DOTs
- **Gadget enemy copies discard boss/drone damage and stuns**:
  - `World.collectGadgetEnemies()` (`server/src/game/bansung/world.ts:3446-3486`) pushes live `MobState` references for regular mobs (`L3450`), but constructs shallow spread copies (`{ ...entry.boss, ... }`, `{ ...d, ... }`, `{ ...aux.target, ... }`) for `auxTargets`, `boss_colossus`, Colossus `drones`, and `boss_overlord` (`L3452-3484`).
  - `stepGadgetWorld` (`world.ts:1939`) mutates `enemy.hp`, `enemy.shield`, `enemy.stunMs`, and `enemy.stunDrStage` on those temporary copies via `applyPairDamageToEnemy` (`server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:1131-1145`), and `reconcileAuxAndBossTargets()` (`world.ts:3158-3229`) only reconciles `this.auxTargets`. Consequently, `stun_bomb`, `cluster_bomb`, and `auto_turret` hits against `boss_colossus`, `drones`, and `boss_overlord` deal zero effective HP/shield damage or stun in live `World` ticks.
- **Sand Beholder `sandProxy` drops stagger (`stunMs`) and newly applied DOTs**:
  - `SandBeholderBoss.combatTarget` (`server/src/game/bansung/boss-sand.ts:511-527`) initializes `ccImmune: true` and leaves `dots` `undefined`. `World.rebuildSpatialGrid()` (`world.ts:1778-1799`) wraps it in a shallow copy `sandProxy` stored in `auxTargets`.
  - `reconcileAuxAndBossTargets()` (`world.ts:3160-3172`) only forwards the HP delta `{ baseDmg: dmg, dmgKind: 'true', critChance: 0 }` to `entry.boss.applyHit(...)`, never forwarding `stunMs` to trigger `addStagger(stunMsToBossStagger(hit.stunMs))` (`boss-sand.ts:589-591`) and never copying `sandProxy.dots` (initialized lazily by `applyDot` at `damage.ts:708-709`) back onto `entry.boss.combatTarget` for `SandBeholderBoss.tickDots()` (`boss-sand.ts:879-889`).
- **Overlord EMP pulse hero stuns are discarded**:
  - `overlordTargets` (`world.ts:2238-2254`) creates shallow spread copies `{ ...(p as BansungCombatTarget), ... }`. Unlike `colossusTargets` (`world.ts:2364-2382`), `stepActiveBosses` never writes `stunMs`, `stunImmuneMs`, or `stunDrStage` back to `p`. Overlord pylon EMP pulses (`server/src/game/bansung/boss-overlord.ts:1166-1172`) mutate only the transient `overlordTargets` entry, leaving live `p.stunMs` unchanged (`0`).

### [MAJOR] 2. `stun_bomb` applies double Stun DR per detonation on mobs; `MIN_SCALED_MS = 150` trips premature 4,000 ms stun immunity on `lightning_staff` hops and weak/falloff stuns
- **Double Stun DR & `mob.stunMult` inconsistency on `stun_bomb`**:
  - `detonateStunBomb` (`server/src/game/bansung/gadgets.ts:1212, 1223-1246`) passes raw `stunTicks` (`66`, ignoring `eliteStunTicks: 42` and `bossStunTicks: 24` in `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:2086-2088`) into `applyPairDamageToEnemy`, which calls `applyBansungDamage` -> `applyStunWithDR(enemy, rawStunMs)` (`server/src/game/bansung/damage.ts:524`) without `mob.stunMult`, advancing `stunDrStage` (`0 -> 1`).
  - When `res.stunAppliedMs > 0`, `detonateStunBomb` invokes `ctx.onStunInterrupt` (`gadgets.ts:1238`), and `World` calls `stunMob(this.mobMgr, mob, info.stunAppliedMs)` (`world.ts:1959-1964`). `stunMob` (`server/src/game/bansung/mobs.ts:1102-1105`) calls `applyStunWithDR` a **second time** in the same tick with `mob.stunMult`, re-applying `TIER_MULT` and `MULTS[1] = 0.5`, jumping `stunDrStage` from `0 -> 2` on a single bomb, and prematurely pushing mobs to stage-2/3 lockout on follow-up CC.
  - Conversely, weapon hits (`weapons.ts:597`) and EMP barrels (`map.ts:829-838`) call `applyBansungDamage` directly rather than `stunMob`, bypassing per-archetype `mob.stunMult` (only `stunMob` passes `entityStunMult`).
- **`MIN_SCALED_MS = 150` turns valid sub-150 ms stuns into immediate 4,000 ms stun immunity (`damage.ts:96-103, 654-663`)**:
  - Because `applyStunWithDR` sets `target.stunImmuneMs = BANSUNG_STUN_DR.IMMUNE_MS` (`4000`) whenever `scaled < 150` (including at `stage = 0`), any stun source whose scaled duration is `< 150 ms` applies `0 ms` stun and grants the target 4 seconds of total stun immunity:
    - `lightning_staff` chain `hopStunMs: 120` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:1606`, `server/src/game/bansung/weapons.ts:2923`): `120 < 150` even at `stage = 0` on a `mob` (`TIER_MULT = 1.0`), so chain hops always deal `0 ms` stun and trigger 4 s stun immunity. Primary `hitStunMs: 280` (`weapons.ts:1829, 3190`) also trips `< 150` at `stage = 1` (`140 ms` mob, `91 ms` elite).
    - `mine_gun` (`stunMs: 350`, `outerFalloffMin: 0.45` at `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:1369, 1371`) on `elite` outer blasts (`~102 ms`) or `stage >= 1` (`elites`: `228 ms` -> `114 < 150`, `mob` outer `< 150`), and partial-charge `railgun_piercer` (`fullStunMs: 450` at `L1551`; `weapons.ts:1942` when `q < 0.34` on mobs or `q < 0.52` on elites) similarly trigger premature 4 s immunity instead of stepping cleanly through `MULTS = [1.0, 0.5, 0.25, 0]`.

### [MAJOR] 3. Uncapped Overlord stun override, permanent Thunder Storm `vulnMult`, and dropped Pet `arcane_ember` burn overrides
- **Overlord EMP stun bypasses `TIER_MULT` / `TIER_MAX_MS.hero` (`1000 ms`)**:
  - `boss-overlord.ts:1166-1172` runs `applyStunWithDR(hero, 1400)` (`42 * 1000/30`), then on `stageBefore === 0` overwrites `hero.stunMs = Math.max(hero.stunMs ?? 0, 1400)`, bypassing `TIER_MULT.hero = 0.7` (`980 ms`) and exceeding `TIER_MAX_MS.hero = 1000` (`damage.ts:102-103`). Conversely, `damageOverlordBoss` (`boss-overlord.ts:1905`) multiplies `hit.stunMs` by `OVERLORD_STATS.stunEffectiveness = 0.45` before `applyBansungDamage` applies `TIER_MULT.boss = 0.35` (`0.1575x` net), while `weapons.ts` and `map.ts` hit `entry.boss` directly via `resolveCombatTarget` (`world.ts:3122`) without `damageOverlordBoss`.
- **Thunder Mage `vulnMs` (`+25%` Shocked damage vulnerability) never expires**:
  - `mobs.ts:2441-2442` sets `tgt.vulnMult = Math.max(tgt.vulnMult ?? 1, 1 + s.stormShockedMul)` and `tgt.vulnMs = Math.max(tgt.vulnMs ?? 0, shockMs)`. `applyBansungDamage` multiplies incoming damage by `target.vulnMult` (`damage.ts:454`), but neither `tickStatuses` (`damage.ts:767-895`) nor `world.ts` ever decrements `vulnMs` or resets `vulnMult` to `1.0`. Once shocked by `thunder_storm`, a player retains permanent vulnerability.
- **Pet `arcane_ember` custom burn DPS & duration are dropped**:
  - `World` (`world.ts:2163, 2179-2181`) spawns pet `arcane_ember` projectiles with `dotKind: 'burn'`, `dotTickDmgOverride: ev.burnDps` (`BANSUNG_PET_TUNING.FIRE_BURN_DPS = 2` in `shared/src/bansung/heroes.ts:576`, missing the `* 0.5` per-500ms-tick factor used by weapons at `weapons.ts:2785, 3205`), and `dotDurMs: Math.round(ev.burnDurS * 1000)` (`1500 ms`).
  - `stepBulletPool` (`weapons.ts:3178-3195`) forwards only `dotKind: b.dotKind` to `applyCombatHitAndEvents` -> `applyBansungDamage` (neither `BansungProjectile` nor `BansungDamageHit` forwards `dotTickDmgOverride` / `dotDurMs`), causing pet embers to apply full default `BANSUNG_DOTS.burn` (`6` dmg/tick for `3000 ms` = `36` total burn damage).

### [MINOR] 4. Spec vs runtime divergences on barrel chain constants, blast falloff, unused DOTs, DOT override stacking, and popup-only flags
- **Barrel chain constants drift between `shared` (`d27`) and `map.ts` (`d06`)**: `BANSUNG_EXPLOSIVE_COOP_RULES` (`shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:1057-1058`) specifies `barrelChainDelayMs: 60` and `barrelMaxChainDepth: 4`, whereas `server/src/game/bansung/map.ts:64-66` uses `BARREL_CHAIN_DELAY_TICKS = 4` (`~133.3 ms`) and `BARREL_MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH = 8`.
- **Barrel AoE falloff vs weapon blast falloff**: Explosive weapons (`weapons.ts:2668-2671`) use `computeBlastFalloff` with a full-damage `coreRadius` (`42–56 px`) and `outerFalloffMin` (`0.45–0.50`, `stunFalloffMin: 0.65` on `stun_launcher`), whereas `detonateBarrel` (`map.ts:758-767, 826`) uses `queryAoeTargets` linear edge falloff `clamp(1 - edgeDist / radius, AOE_MIN_FALLOFF, 1)` (`spatial.ts:29, 1815-1817`, `AOE_MIN_FALLOFF = 0.35`, no inner core plateau).
- **Unused `BANSUNG_DOTS` (`shock`, `poison`, `bleed`) & `tickDmgOverride` stacking**: `damage.ts` implements `burn`, `poison` (`+15%` vuln), `shock` (`96 px` `0.5x` chain), and `bleed` (`1.5x` moving at `speed > 20`), but no weapon, gadget, barrel, mob, or boss applies `poison`, `shock`, or `bleed` DOTs (`burn` only). `BANSUNG_DMG_SHOCK` (`fxFlags |= 32`, `damage.ts:541`) only fires on `hit.isDot && hit.dmgKind === 'energy'`, so electric weapons and `barrel_emp` never set it. Additionally, `applyDot` (`damage.ts:712-720`) + `tickStatuses` (`844-847`) increment `stacks` (`<= 3`) without scaling when `tickDmgOverride` is set, and a weaker `tickDmgOverride` (`plasma_rifle` `4`/tick or `pyro_shaman` `2`/tick) overwrites a stronger active override (`arcane_book` `6`/tick).
- **Popup-only `IMMUNE` / `DOT` flags omitted from `World`**: `World.buildDamageContext()` (`world.ts:3033-3038`) omits `popups`, so `BANSUNG_DMG_FLAG_IMMUNE` (`8`) and `BANSUNG_DMG_FLAG_DOT` (`16`) pushed in `damage.ts:534, 549-550, 661, 688, 698` are never consumed or replicated via wire `FX_DMG`.

## Verified Invariants

- **Core Damage, Affinity & Shield Soak (`server/src/game/bansung/damage.ts:67-93, 335-555`)**: `calcBansungDamage` accurately implements the 7-kind Miragine affinity matrix, `pierce`/`true` 100% `flatArmor` ignore, kinetic crit `+2` armor pen (`BANSUNG_KINETIC_CRIT_ARMOR_PEN`), `0.85` crit cap, `1.25x` shield bonus for `energy`/`explosive`, `Strong Shield` break overflow absorb (`rem = 0`), `250 ms` HP i-frames, `280 ms` dash i-frames, `3200 ms` / `2200 ms` shield regen delay reset, and `750 ms` `+10` shield regen ticks.
- **Stun DR & DOT Engine Core (`server/src/game/bansung/damage.ts:95-126, 636-895`)**: `BANSUNG_STUN_DR` enforces `MULTS = [1.0, 0.5, 0.25, 0]`, `10_000 ms` (`12_000 ms` boss) decay reset, `4000 ms` lockout, `2500 ms` hero post-stun immunity, and per-tier multipliers/caps; `applyDot`/`tickStatuses` enforce `fire_ward`/`toxin_ward` immunities and 500 ms DOT intervals bypassing non-hero shields.
- **Barrel Specs, Co-op Safety & Respawn (`server/src/game/bansung/map.ts:393-443, 513-515, 665-962, 1097-1170`, `director.ts:1816`, `world.ts:1679, 2572-2574`)**: `barrel_explosive` (`140` explosive, `160 px`) and `barrel_emp` (`120` energy + `1200 ms` stun, `170 px`) enforce LOS occlusion, radial knockback (`260` mob / `120` player), and `18` shield-only chip damage on players (`applyShieldOnlyChip`, respecting i-frames and resetting regen delay); `stepBarrelDetonations` is wired into `World` and wave/survival prop respawns mark `propsDirty = true`.
- **Explosive Friendly-Fire & Burn Conversions**: Player explosives (`weapons.ts:2663, 2697-2715, 2628-2644`) skip friendly damage, apply `35%` non-lethal self-knockback, and enforce `rocketMultiHitDecay` clamps; `arcane_book` (`weapons.ts:2785`), `plasma_rifle` crits (`weapons.ts:3205`), and `pyro_shaman` fireballs (`mobs.ts:2500`) correctly halve per-second `burnDps` for 500 ms DOT ticks.
