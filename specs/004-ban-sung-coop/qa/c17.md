# QA Audit Report — c17: Miragine & Sprite Build Pipeline

## Scope

Cross-checked the full `ban_sung` sprite normalization & build pipeline (`scripts/bansung/reuse-miragine.mjs`, `scripts/bansung/build-hero-sprites.mjs`, `scripts/bansung/build-fx-sprites.mjs`, `scripts/bansung/build-mob-sprites.mjs`, `scripts/bansung/pack-manifest.mjs`, and shared helpers in `scripts/bansung/lib-pack.mjs`) against all `230` unique PNGs in `database-ban-sung/normalized/**`, `database-ban-sung/manifest.json` (`432` sprite keys), `database-ban-sung/sprites/**/meta-*.json`, `database-ban-sung/sources.json`, `database-ban-sung/LICENSES.md`, `database-miraginewar/mapping.json`, and runtime shared/client consumers (`shared/src/bansung/{heroes,weapons,enemies,maps}.ts`, `client/src/bansung/{sprites,anim,render,vfx}.ts`). Zero production source or asset files were modified.

## Cross-Check Findings

### BLOCKER
- **NONE** — Baseline asset gates pass (`pack-manifest.mjs --check` and `verify-assets.mjs`), all `432` sprite keys resolve to `230` valid unique PNGs under `database-ban-sung/normalized/**`, and every strip's IHDR matches `frames * cellW x cellH` with `0` blank frames.

### MAJOR

1. **`tile_desert_floor` / `tile_desert_trim` contain transparent cutouts and off-biome purple/pink pixels, `tile_crypt_floor` flattens to 1 solid color, and tile LUT blending (`lutStrength: 0.75`) corrupts `#181425` to `#291b1e`**
   - **Files:** `scripts/bansung/build-mob-sprites.mjs:622,2172-2188,2221,2236-2244,3274-3298`; `scripts/bansung/lib-pack.mjs:678,711` -> `database-ban-sung/normalized/tiles/{tile_desert_floor,tile_desert_trim,tile_crypt_floor,tile_crypt_wall,tile_cyber_floor,tile_cyber_wall,tile_cyber_trim}.png`; `client/src/bansung/render.ts:1101-1103`
   - **Detail:**
     - `tile_desert_floor` scales `kenney_desert_shooter/Tiles/Tiles/tile_0000.png` directly via `makeSeamless48TileFromSample` without filling transparency or remapping non-sand colors. The emitted `48x48` PNG (`2304` px) has **`126` transparent pixels** (`alpha === 0`) and off-palette blue/purple/pink pixels (`#9196bb`: 1373 px, `#3c224a`: 531 px, `#f0a1e5`: 261 px, `#934ca1`: 9 px, only `2` px `#c9885b`), creating transparent grid gaps and pink/purple blotches across the Desert floor (`render.ts:1101`). `tile_desert_trim` clones `desertFloor` underneath (`88` transparent px + `#3c224a`/`#f0a1e5`/`#9196bb`).
     - `tile_crypt_floor` bucket-remaps `kenney_tiny_dungeon/Tiles/tile_0048.png` by luminance (`< 90`, `< 150`, else `#3a4466`), flattening all `2304` pixels into a single uniform `#3a4466` rectangle with zero stone detail.
     - `emitStrip` runs `quantizeImageToPalette48(..., { lutStrength: 0.75, preserveOutline: false })` on all 9 tiles while `getDefaultPaletteLut()` calls `buildPaletteLut(BANSUNG_PALETTE_RGBA, 1)`, which omits palette index `0` (`#181425`). Consequently every `C.NAVY_DEEP` (`#181425`) mortar/seam pixel blends 75% toward `#2e1d1b` into off-palette `#291b1e` across `tile_crypt_wall` (599 px), `tile_cyber_wall` (576 px), `tile_cyber_floor` (95 px), and `tile_cyber_trim` (372 px).

2. **Weapon `muzzleOffset` vs `pivot` coordinate-frame inconsistency and `WEAPON_RIG_SPECS` pivot drift across `build-fx-sprites.mjs`, `pack-manifest.mjs`, `weapons.ts`, and `anim.ts`**
   - **Files:** `scripts/bansung/build-fx-sprites.mjs:2145-2398`; `database-ban-sung/sprites/fx/meta-fx.json`; `scripts/bansung/pack-manifest.mjs:156-165`; `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts:1505,1557,1612,1664,1755,1806,1857`; `client/src/bansung/anim.ts:199-215`
   - **Detail:**
     - Kinetic/heavy guns and gadgets store **grip-relative** `muzzleOffset = [muzzleX - anchorX, muzzleY - anchorY]` (e.g., `gun_pistol`: `anchor=(6,8)`, `muzzle=(23,6)` -> `[17,-2]`). Energy weapons in `build-fx-sprites.mjs` and `shared/src/bansung/weapons.ts` instead store top-left cell coordinates in `muzzleOffset`: `plasma_rifle` `[30, 6]` vs grip-relative `[22, -4]`, `railgun_piercer` `[36, 7]` vs `[27, -3]`, `lightning_staff` `[34, 7]` vs `[24, 0]`, and `arcane_book` `[16, 0]` vs `[8, 0]` (`muzzleX=18, anchorX=10`), while melee weapons store `[0, -14]` in `weapons.ts` vs `[32,0]`/`[40,-10]`/`[30,0]` in `meta-fx.json`.
     - `pack-manifest.mjs` `sanitizePivot` strips `ejectX`, `ejectY`, `shoulderOffsetX`, `shoulderLiftY`, and `shoulderR` when building `manifest.json` (`pivot: { anchorX, anchorY, muzzleX?, muzzleY? }`), leaving `client/src/bansung/anim.ts` `WEAPON_RIG_SPECS` hardcoded out-of-sync with `meta-fx.json` on 14 of 15 weapons (e.g., `eject` off by 1px on 10 guns and `(10,7)` vs `(28,7)` on `lightning_staff`; `shoulderR` mismatched on 13 weapons, notably `bazooka` `10` vs `5` and `arcane_book` `10` vs `16`).

3. **Miragine unit processing clips 1px outlines at cell borders and creates `#2e1d1b` double-borders; `vfx_thunderball` (`thunderball`) `loop: false` freezes active projectiles on frame 15**
   - **Files:** `scripts/bansung/reuse-miragine.mjs:188-197,362-375`; `scripts/bansung/pack-manifest.mjs:533,554-556`; `client/src/bansung/sprites.ts:360,839-840`; `client/src/bansung/vfx.ts:385,388,3401-3405` -> `database-ban-sung/normalized/{heroes,enemies,bosses}/miragine_*.png`, `normalized/projectiles/proj_thunderball.png`
   - **Detail:**
     - All 28 flipped Miragine unit strips (`miragine_a0_*..miragine_b7_*`) run `quantizeImageToPalette48(..., { preserveOutline: false })` -> `applyOutline4Conn` without `stripDarkOutline` or edge padding. Because `buildPaletteLut(..., 1)` omits `#181425`, original black outlines/ink (`<=19` RGB, up to `1,063` px in `miragine_a11_attack`) quantize to `#2e1d1b` (`R2_EARTH[0]`), after which `applyOutline4Conn` adds a second `#181425` border around them. Moreover, all 28 source sheets already touch cell edges (`3..79` opaque source pixels on `x=0`/`cellW-1`/`y=0`/`cellH-1`), clipping outer `#181425` outline pixels and leaving `13..85` `#181425` pixels directly on frame boundaries.
     - `reuse-miragine.mjs:373` emits `vfx_thunderball` (`16f @ 128x128, 18fps`) with `loop: false`, which `pack-manifest.mjs` aliases to `thunderball` and `proj_thunderball`. `vfx.ts:385,388,3401-3405` draws `thunder_orb` and `turret_thunder` bullets with `clipKey: 'thunderball'` and `animTimeSec: nowSec`; `resolveFrameIndex` (`sprites.ts:839-840`) clamps non-looping clips to frame `15` after `~0.89s` of uptime (unlike `sprites.ts:360` fallback metadata which sets `loop: true`).

### MINOR

1. **`pack-manifest.mjs` silent metadata drops/coercions and `preserveDarkOutline` option typo**

   - `scripts/bansung/build-fx-sprites.mjs`: 10 `4conn`-outlined weapon/projectile strips touch frame boundaries (`gun_rifle`: L=4; `gun_smg`: L=1; `w_frag_launcher`: L=6/R=6; `w_stun_launcher`: L=4/R=5; `w_triple_bazooka`: L=8/R=3; `plasma_rifle`: L=4/R=3; `railgun_piercer`: R=4; `lightning_staff`: R=12; `bullet_kinetic_md`: L=8/R=8; `p_stun_canister`: R=4/B=4), as do `pack-manifest.mjs` portraits `ui_portrait_{mascot,knight,pyro,ranger}.png` (`32x32`, `lrtb`).

3. **`pk_xp_orb` unused normalized sheet & pickup anchor mismatch; `BANSUNG_PROJECTILE_CONFIGS` FPS drift**
   - `scripts/bansung/build-mob-sprites.mjs:3115-3129` builds `pk_xp_orb` as `16x16` (`4f`, `pivotMode: 'feet'`, `off=(-8,-14)`), differing from the other 7 `32x32` center-anchored `pk_*` pickups (`(-16,-16)`), while `client/src/bansung/render.ts:1688-1691` draws procedural diamonds (`drawXpCrystalDiamond`) and never renders `pk_xp_orb`.

4. **Off-palette (`23` PNGs) & semi-alpha (`12` PNGs) exceptions lack manifest tags**
   - Beyond the 6 broken tile PNGs in Major #1, `17` normalized VFX/Miragine raw PNGs contain off-`BANSUNG_PALETTE_48` colors and `12` contain soft alpha (`a_crystal`, `vfx_fireball`, `vfx_storm`, `vfx_thunderball`, `fx_blast_he`, `fx_blast_stun`, `fx_crater_decals`, `fx_dash_dust`, `fx_explosion_small`, `fx_muzzle_kinetic`, `fx_slash_crescent`, `fx_slash_scythe`, `fx_slash_shock`, `fx_smoke_puff`, `fx_stun_ring`, `impact_spark`, `muzzle_flash`, `muzzle_plasma`, `plasma_bolt`, `rail_beam`, `ui_shadow_unit`). Most are intentional `outlineMode: 'none'` additive/OSS assets, but dropping `outline` from `manifest.json` prevents validators from separating intentional soft-alpha/FX exceptions from tile defects.

5. **`sources.json` omits `generated_synth` / `internal_miragine` and `build-hero-sprites.mjs` CC0 refs are metadata-only**
   - All `15` unique `sourceId` values in `manifest.json` are documented in `database-ban-sung/LICENSES.md`, whereas `generated_synth` and `internal_miragine` are absent from `database-ban-sung/sources.json`.
   - `build-hero-sprites.mjs` downloads 7 CC0 PNGs into an OS temp directory to record `cc0References` in `meta-heroes.json`, while drawing all 24 hero and 8 pet strips procedurally (`sourceId: 'generated_synth'`).

## Verdict: FAIL

`pack-manifest.mjs --check` and `verify-assets.mjs` pass cleanly on structural manifest integrity (`432` sprite keys, `230` PNGs), but pixel and cross-module verification uncovered **4 MAJOR** defects affecting live gameplay rendering and shared contracts (Major #1: `tile_desert_floor`/`tile_desert_trim` transparent cutouts & pink/purple palette, flat 1-color `tile_crypt_floor`, and `#291b1e` LUT shift; Major #2: `plasma_rifle`/`railgun_piercer`/`lightning_staff`/`arcane_book` `muzzleOffset` vs `pivot` mismatch + `WEAPON_RIG_SPECS` pivot drift; Major #3: `reuse-miragine.mjs` double-border/edge-clipped outlines + `thunderball` `loop: false` freezing projectiles on frame 15; Major #4: `shared/src/bansung/enemies.ts` stale Miragine dimensions/facings) plus **5 MINOR** findings.
